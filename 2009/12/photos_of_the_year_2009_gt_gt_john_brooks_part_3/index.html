Photos Of The Year 2009&gt;&gt; John Brooks Part 2

December 4, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Le Mans 24 Hours, Le Mans, France

Whatever the reverse of altitude sickness is, (the Bends?) then these three musketeers must have had it bad on the podium at this year's Les Vingt Quatre Heures du Mans. TK, in particular, is more accustomed to the dizzy heights of the top spot having graced that exclusive piece of turf an incredible EIGHT times. So third place was something of a come down. How else do you explain why he would spend his time pointing out an old photographer in the pen that the ACO generously provide for our protection against the rampaging hoards. Nishy has his usual sneer………Dindo is very happy………….they will be back on top next year. I'll still be in the pen……………

Bonneville Speed Week, Bonneville Salt Flats, Utah, USA

A special time, a special place, 06.27AM, Bonneville Salt Flats, Utah

Nish Motorsports prepare to go for a run, 400mph is their target, real speed………………….

LMS Test Days, Circuit Paul Ricard, France

First public outing for the Lola Aston Martin LMP1 at the High Tec Test Track, Le Castellet, South of France, along the Mistral Straight, running on rails…………..V12 pulling like a freight train………hear the lonesome whistle blow…………

Le Mans 24 Hours, Le Mans, France

Homer Simpson goes to La Sarthe……………. I wonder what the French for DOH! is? Le DOH!? Two Peugeots hitting each other during the second set of pit stops, not their finest hour but that was the last mistake the French team would make on their way to a deserved victory in the Le Mans 24 Hours.

AMOC Autumn Concours, Mapledurham House, Reading, UK

The original Aston Martin Owners' Club badge………………..

Nurburgring 24 Hours, Nurburgring, Germany

On the way down from Brunnchen to Pflanzgarten, a GT3 Alpina in the Nurburgring 24 Hours…………crazy event, utterly mad place.

Sebring 12 Hours, Sebring, Florida, USA

Sebring, Sunset, Spray, Silhouette, Super………………….

Le Mans 24 Hours, Le Mans, France

Pescarolo Sport Peugeot 908 Hdi-FAP into Porsche Curves on Saturday evening, it would be destroyed some time around 4.00AM the next morning…………….

Spyker Aileron Shoot, Iseo, Italy

Spyker on the streets of Italy, as cool as a Cappuccino………………..

AMOC Autumn Concours, Mapledurham House, Reading, UK

Aston Martin V12 Vantage on the lawn fresh from a class win in the Nurburgring 24 Hours.
