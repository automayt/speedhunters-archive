Photos Of The Year 2009&gt;&gt;mike&#039;s Shots Pt.3

December 16, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Are we really just two weeks from the start of 2010? It may not feel like it, but indeed it's time to cross another year off the list. Over the past few days I've started putting together the my rough 2010 schedule, and it should be another awesome year. But before we move into 2010 we've got to finish our look back at 2009.

Here's my final batch of '09 Photos of the Year.

Monterey Sports Championships, Laguna Seca Raceway, Monterey, California. The Cork Screw changed my life…

JCCA New Year Meeting, Tokyo Japan. A pile of vintage car parts for sale at the JCCA New Year Meeting. This reminds me that I need to get out to the Pomona and Long Beach auto swap meets soon.

Garage Boso, Gardena, California. An S14 sitting a couple yards from a bunch of lowriders. Man, I love Southern California.

HKS Premium Day , Fuji Speedway, Shizuoka-ken Japan. An R34 GT-R runs at Fuji Speedway during the annual HKS premium day.

The Hot Rod Reunion, Famoso Raceway, Bakersfield California. Some vintage funny car magic thanks to Don the Snake Prudhomme.

Formula Drift Round Two, Road Atlanta, Braselton, Georgia. Taka Aono gets dirty in Atlanta.

The Cruisin' Nationals, Santa Maria, California. Living the kustom life at the Cruisin' Nationals.

Japanese Classic Car Show, Irvine, California. Datsun 510's bask in the sun at JCCS.

Drift Soukoukai, Tsukuba Circuit, Ibaraki-ken Japan. A little grassroots drift action at Tsukuba Circuit.

SoCal Bug-In 34, Autoclub Speedway, Fontana, CA. A ratted and slammed VW at Bug-In 34.

YMS Cup, Nikko Circuit, Tochigi-ken, Japan.  This is actually one of my favorite drifting photos I've ever taken.

Long Beach Grand Prix, Long Beach, California. The Falken GT2 Porsche debuts on the streets of the LBC.

Nisei Showoff, Downtown Los Angeles, California. A wave of color on a group of Honda Civics at the Nisei Showoff in downtown LA,

Formula Drift ProAm, Evergreen Speedway, Monroe, Washington. The PNW drift community is second to none.

X-Games 15, Carson, California. Dave Mirra brings her down nice and gently at the Home Depot Center.

Idlers Games, Tsukuba Circuit, Ibaraki-ken. Tsukuba Circuit, I can't quit you!

Mooneyes X-Mas Party, Irwindale Spedway, Irwindale, California. One of the reasons I waited to make this post was because I knew there'd be something cool to add from last weekend's X-Mas Party.

Super Lap Battle Finals, Buttonwillow Raceway, Buttonwillow, California. JC Meynet at speed in the JHPUSA S2000.

All Star Bash '09, Willow Springs Raceway, Rosamond, CA. Mr. Chobs goes west.

Redline Time Attack, Buttonwillow Raceway, Buttonwillow, California. This is one of those California moments.

OK that does it for my Photos of the Year contributions. Hope you enjoyed this look back over the past 365 as much as I did.

-Mike Garrett
