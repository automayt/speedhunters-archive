Video Roll&gt;&gt; Touge Heroes: The Remix

July 17, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Although the email form on our homepage here seems to have stopped working, I still get a lot of emails and facebook messages from all corners of the globe with offers, suggestions and video links for Speedhunters. It’s quite exciting to think that so many of you are enjoying the site and want to collaborate with us!

I got this link passed to me a couple days ago from UK based automotive film-maker Al Clark, the creator of the Touge Heroes DVD. He’s made a 20 minute remix of the DVD which strings together some of the best bits from the main film. My favourite parts are the opening sequence with the R32 and the shots of the Driftworks S15 hitting the “touge” in the UK… I’m less hot on the Red Bull footage, which to my eye drags on a bit too much. It’s still worth a look though, you won’t be disappointed!

It’s quite interesting to see the different drift and automotive film-makers emerging from the woodworks around the world and I’m sure that the Touge Heroes crew will be leading the way in the UK scene for the coming years.

BTW if you have tried to contact Speedhunters in the past 2 months it’s likely that the email has gotten lost… best to email rchong (at) ea.com if you have any proposals, comments, complaints or suggestions you want to run past us.

:R
