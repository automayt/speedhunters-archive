July 17, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Just as the sun rises above the horizon, Marcus Gronholm's all-wheel drive, 800 horse power, Ford Fiesta plows by my photo spot from this morning's practice and qualifying session on Pike's Peak. It was an absolutely gorgeous morning accompanied by an symphony of monsterous sounds coming from the unlimited class. Stay tuned for more coverage of the Pike's Peak International Hill Climb!
