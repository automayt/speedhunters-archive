Random Snap&gt;&gt; 993 Gt2 Track Day Weapon

March 12, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

I just spotted this shot on Flickr in Yori Langhenkel's photostream. He seems to be a young Dutch photographer with a taste for hot transportation appliances like this beautiful 993 GT2. According to the photo caption, this picture was shot near Nurburg…. which can only mean one thing: the owner likes to flog this sexy beast on the Nurburgring Nordschleife…

Wow….can you imagine what it must be like to be an position to own a car like this, let alone drive it on the world's most fearsome race track?…. hmmm perhaps it's best not to think about such things, one will only get depressed!
