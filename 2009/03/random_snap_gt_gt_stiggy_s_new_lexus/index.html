Random Snap&gt;&gt; Stiggy&#039;s New Lexus

March 5, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Because we are going to look at new car builds for different series around the world, I thought it would be cool to show you this new drift Lexus. It is owned by Steve 'Stiggy' Evans from England, and it's almost finished. I can't say too much now, but expect a full feature soon.

The car currently sits on standard cut springs and shocks so be gentle on the weak offset!
