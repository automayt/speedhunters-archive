Desktops&gt;&gt;march 2009 Calendar

March 2, 2009 Andy Blackmore

The majority of Motor Racing Championships begin during the month of March, so we have chosen to celebrate, Audi Sport, Sebring 12 Hours and the American Le Mans Series on this month's Calendar.

The 1999 Sebring 12 hrs was the very first ALMS
race. We chose John Brooks cool night image of  'Dindo' Capello driving the Audi R8R, which was making its debut at the event. The start of a long successful series of Audi LMP cars.

Fast forward, back to this month…. Audi will use this month's Sebring race to debut their latet machine, the
Audi R15 TDI LMP1 car. Dindo is due to drive Car #2 with two other
sports car legends, Allan McNish and Tom Kristensen.

The calendar is available in two sizes in the Destops section of Speedhunters.com
