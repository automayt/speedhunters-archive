Video&gt;&gt; Truth In 24 Trailer

January 16, 2009 Andy Blackmore

Audi of America and NFL Films recently produced a stunning documentary, ‘Truth in 24′.

The documentary follows the three car Audi Sport team at last year’s 24 hours of Le Mans. The file is narrated by British Actor Jason Statham. ESPN in the United States will be broadcasting the 96 minute film on Friday 20th March, the day before Sebring. The film has already won the Top Documentary Prize at the Fort Lauderdale International Film Festival in 2008 and will also be shown at the Santa Barbara Film Festival later this month.

You can download a higher resolution of the trailer at the (very impressive) official website. Can’t wait for this, assuming they show it here in Canada.
