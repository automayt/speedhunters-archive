Video Roll&gt;&gt; Dakar Day 13 Review

January 16, 2009 Andy Blackmore

The Red Bull Volkswagen Team took it easy today to ensure their 1-2 lead remained intact, without problems. The lead VW’s stopped early in the stage to wait for their team mate, Dieter Depping so they could run safely in formation. This allowed Joan Roma to take the first stage win for the Mitsubishi Lancer on this year’s rally.

Robbie Gordon lost some time due to a throttle problem, but he is in a very comfortable third.
