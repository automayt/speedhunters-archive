Random Snap&gt;&gt; Voltex 350z

January 6, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

I was just browsing through the Voltex blog and found this 350Z with their latest spoiler design. I have seen them test fitting this design on the new EVO X and I wasn't very fond of how it looked. But seeing it on this 350Z has changed my mind. Those AME TM-02 wheels also help to enhance the overall look of the car, gorgeous.

-Jeroen Willemsen
