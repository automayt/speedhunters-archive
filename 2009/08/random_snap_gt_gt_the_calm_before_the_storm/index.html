Random Snap&gt;&gt; The Calm Before The Storm

August 8, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

You can feel that there is an air of suspense throughout the Formula D paddock during Friday's practice and qualifying. And for some reason you just feel that today's top thirty-two event will somehow go down into the history books as an epic event. With a 30% chance of rain, it could spell trouble for some teams or be a complete blessing. Personally, I'm hoping it to just absolute pour down hard for some, hopefully, awesome photos of FormulaD in the rain!

So stay tuned because there's a lot more Formula D Seattle coverage coming your way.

-Linhbergh Nguyen
