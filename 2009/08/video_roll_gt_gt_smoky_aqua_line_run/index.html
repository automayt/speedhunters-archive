Video Roll&gt;&gt; Smoky Aqualine Run

After seeing the BNR34 Competizione-R in action on the Wangan, I thought it would be cool to see Nagata-san himself blasting down the Aqualine at 328 km/h with his 1200 PS BCNR33. Watch him pull a proper drag launch in the middle of tunnel as Fly from Max Power snaps away with his camera. Legendary video!
