News&gt;&gt;audi Announce Nurburgring Quartet

April 22, 2009 Andy Blackmore

Audi have announced factory support for four Audi R8 LMS entries in May's Nurburgring 24 Hour Race. ABT Sportsline and Phoenix Racing will run two cars each at the endurance event. The #100 car pictured above looks great with the race number used to celebrate 100th Anniversary of the Audi brand.

Audi will be supplying a number of their works drivers to the event. The driving line up is amazing!

#97 ABT Sportsline

Christian Abt, Jean-Francois Hemroulle, Pierre Kaffer, Lucas Luhr

#98 Phoenix Racing

Marc Besseng, Frank Stippler, Mike Rockenfeller

#99 Phoenix Racing

Frank Biela, Marcel Fassler, Emanuele Pirro, Hans-Joachim Stuck

#100 ABT Sportsline

Mattias Ekstrom, Timo Scheider, Marco Werner

-Andy Blackmore
