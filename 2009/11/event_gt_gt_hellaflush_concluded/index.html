Event&gt;&gt;hellaflush Concluded

November 19, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

OK, now to wrap up my coverage from last weekend's Hellaflush III meet in Nor Cal. Was it really only five days ago? It seems like so much longer than that. Goes to show how crazy this month has been.

Anyways, on with the photos!

Remember what I said about the S2000 turnout at the meet? Here's just part of the lineup.

This dumped G35 Coupe was another one of the cars that made the road trip from Southern California.

It's always nice to see some Z32 love. I think the body lines of these cars make them perfect for aggressive wheel fitment and a healthy drop.

Another view of the white FD3S I posted the other day. I've had me eye on a set of Work VS-KF's for a while now – they seem to look good on everything really.

This EG Civic looks just right. And what's that car beside it?

An A31 Cefiro from Japan. Cool. In places like Australia, New Zealand, and even Canada it's not a big deal to see one these, but here in California we tend to get a little excited over them.

And now, three vastly different cars wearing BBS RS wheels. First up we have this "bugeye" WRX wagon.

Next up, a roof-racked MKIV Volkswagen Jetta.

And finally a Scion xB with a very unique color combination. This just goes to show the variety of cars that came out for this meet.

It doesn't get any cleaner than this black zenki S14 on Weds Kranze Ratzingers.

Nice looking second generation Honda Odyssey. Vanning geeks will know that this model was sold in Japan as the Honda LaGreat.

Not hella flush, but hella low.

One more Scion xB with surprise…a Yakima roof rack!

I'm a big fan of the old school Stern wheels on this box.

This white-silver RX8 also caught my eye.

You'd expect to see more RX8's done like this, but they are heavily outnumbered by their piston-powered rivals from Nissan and Infniti.

Fatlace Mark's Porsche, perhaps taking some inspiration from all the awesome Japanese-built Porsches we've featured here?

Another shot of the tire stretch on Sean Williams' E36 M3.

OK then, that does it for my Hellaflush III coverage. Thanks to Fatlace and Blacktrax for throwing one hell of car meet.

Stay tuned next week for a couple car spotlights from the event.

-Mike Garrett
