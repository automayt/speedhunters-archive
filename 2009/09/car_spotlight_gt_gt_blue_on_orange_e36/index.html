Car Spotlight&gt;&gt;blue On Orange E36

September 9, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

The other day I showed you a random snap of an E46 M3 convertible from the East Coast's Digitul Heaven group. Coming from the same group is this two door E36 M3 owned by Edward Chen of Potomac, Maryland. Like the E46, this Estoril Blue M3 was originally built for winding road fun, but Edward has since become interested in taking his BMW out drifting.

The car's modification list includes Ground Control suspension, TC Kline camber plates, F1 racing clutch and flywheel. UUC exhaust and tranny mounts, and Euro headlights.

And then there's the wheels – Fikse FM10's 17×8 front and17x 8.5 rear with a +20 offset. The centers of the wheels have been coated in a custom orange color stolen from the Lambhorghini catalog, resulting in a nice contrast with the OEM blue paint.

And finally a shot of a little sideways action, no doubt on a closed race course…

Thanks for the photos Edward!

-Mike Garrett
