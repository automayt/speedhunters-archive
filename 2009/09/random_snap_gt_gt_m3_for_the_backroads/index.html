Random Snap&gt;&gt;m3 For The Backroads

September 7, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

We are only one week into BMW month, but already we've had a tremendous response from our readers and the BMW community in general. One of the submissions we got is this E46 M3 'vert owned by a Maryland-based reader who goes by the name "Digitul Heaven". He says that the car is used primarily for runs down the local backroads and mountain passes. Mods include full suspension with F2AutoLife coilovers and staggered 19" DPE wheels.

Keep the cool BMW's coming guys! Send pics and a bit of info about the car to Rchong (at) ea.com and we'll try to get it up.

-Mike Garrett
