Random Snap &gt;&gt; E39 M5 Powered E30

September 14, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

I spotted this car last year whilst at the Swedish round of the Prodrift European Series. It all looks very ordinary, but under the hood lies an S62 4.9L V8 pumping out 400 horsepower. On the flight back I was talking to our guy who works the startline and when the car first rolled up, he thought the car was down a cylinder (not knowing about the V8 conversion) He signalled this to the driver who smiled and replied that it wasn't down one; but up four.

- Paddy
