Random Snap&gt;&gt; Lola Aston Martin Lmp1 In London

September 3, 2009 Jonathan Moore

Aston Martin are helping publicise the upcoming 1000kms Le Mans Series race at Silverstone by displaying the 007 car outside Euston station in London for two days this week, so I had to drop by to get a picture. They'll be three Lola Aston Martins at Silverstone, with their clean sweep of the Nurburgring podium positions to live up to. It's going to be a tough ask…

The car was drawing a lot of attention from passers-by, though there weren't really a lot of obvious signs as to why it was there! Not the best piece of marketing effort. But still, they were playing the iconic Steve McQueen Le Mans film on a screen in the awning by the side of it. A fantastic car and a reminder that we'll have coverage of the race here on Speedhunters.
