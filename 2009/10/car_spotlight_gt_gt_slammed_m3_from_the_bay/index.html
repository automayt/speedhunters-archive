Car Spotlight&gt;&gt;slammed M3 From The Bay

October 1, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

As Speedhunters' BMW month finishes up we have one more Stanceworks BMW to showcase, and one look at the car will tell you why we saved it for last.

This E36 M3 is owned by Stanceworks member Sean Williams of San Jose, California. Despite the ridiculously low stance, Sean uses the M3 as his daily driver.

To get the slammed look ("pretty much the lowest E36 on the planet" Mike Burroughs writes), Sean uses a set of H&R Coilovers modified for maximum lowness.

And then there's the wheels of course – BBS RS 212's in 17×8.5 +13 all around.

Although standard RS's would have been fine, Sean decided to set the wheels off with custom white centers and bright green barrels. Looks a bit like a BMW Art Car doesn't it?

Again, thanks to Mike Burroughs for letting us tap into his archive of sexy BMW photos from the Stanceworks crew this month!

-Mike Garrett
