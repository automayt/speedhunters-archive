Need For Speed Shift&gt;&gt; Demo Release And Update Information

October 1, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

I know some of you wanted to find out information about what technical issues with the game are being dealt with by the Need for Speed SHIFT production team. I've been given this link here which shows the main issues that are currently being worked on. I hope that helps!

Vaughn Gittin JR is also working on a tutorial for the drift mode which he's going to post here early next week. He told me today that some people within his drifting crowd are starting to get the hang of the mode which is great news. But I'll let him talk to you about that directly.

In the meantime I wanted to show you this bolted on overfender that SMS modelled so nicely on the S13!

I also never got a chance to show you this "ode to Matt Powers" S14 that I did up a few weeks ago.

BTW If any of you want to try out Need for Speed SHIFT for yourself, you can download the free demo for Xbox 360 and Playstation 3 as of today. You can sample 5 cars: a Lotus Elise, Nissan GT-R, Dodge Viper, BMW M3 and the Pagani Zonda R (once you've unlocked it) on two tracks: London River and Spa-Francorchamps.

Make sure to drive in the cockpit view for the full experience and turn off all the driver aides to get the full effect!
