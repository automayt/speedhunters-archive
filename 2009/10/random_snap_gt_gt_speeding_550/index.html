Random Snap&gt;&gt; Speeding 550

October 29, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

Some of you should recognize this car as the 550 Maranello that has been build by Garage Saurus. It's a car that is constant developing. I have been following the build and I have been amazed at the quality of the build. This time they went out for a track day on the Fuji Speedway for some much needed testing. It is just a testament that Garage Saurus is still one of Japans greatest tuners.
