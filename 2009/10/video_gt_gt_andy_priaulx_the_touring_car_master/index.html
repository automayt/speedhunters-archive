Video&gt;&gt; Andy Priaulx: The Touring Car Master

October 30, 2009 TheSpeedhunters

This Speedhunters film is going to be my final contribution to the Driver’s Month special features.

It’s a quick portrait of the multiple World Touring Car Champion, Andy Priaulx.

Shot last month at the Imola round of the WTCC, we’ve done our best to capture the essence of Touring Car racing. The intensity, the aggressive driving and the on track dramatics.

Thanks Andy Priaulx for taking the time to talk to us. Enjoy the film!
