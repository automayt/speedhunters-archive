Random Snap&gt;&gt; Look Deep Into Your Eyes....

March 12, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

The thing I love about Formula D is that it's media has a lot of freedom to keep pushing their respective disciplines' boundaries. For that very reason, FD has become a breeding ground for young creatives whom aspire to become professionals. 

On the other hand, there are professionals like Allison Merion, who is also looking to improve her skill and push her creative boundaries. This sweet shot of Ross Petty with a remote flash mounted inside the car is an amazing example of just that.
