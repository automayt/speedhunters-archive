Random Snap&gt;&gt; The Beauty Of The Garden State

June 7, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Most of the people on the FD grid completely dislike going to the Wall Speedway. Everyone refers to it as "the toilet bowl" for the fact that when it rains, water has no where to go but to just sit in the bowl waiting to evaporate. But they all have good reason not to like the track; it is the most run down looking stop on the FD calendar, it is the stop on the calendar that has the least amount of spectators, the asphalt is literally falling apart, and the transition from the banking into the infield ruins cars.

I beg to differ. I love Wall Speedway for the fact that its just so photogenic. The whole track is shootable from any angle, there are trees in the background and fantastic cloud formations that constantly change. But I am a just mere photographer in the much bigger landscape of FD….
