News&gt;&gt; Sumo Power Gt-r To Guest At Modified Live

June 14, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

I've just received some great news from Andy Barnes letting us know that the Sumo Power GT-R GT1 will be present at the Modified Live event on the 27th of June at Brands Hatch where it will be doing some demonstration runs before the crowd throughout the day. The same event will also be host to the third round of the Time Attack Series and the European Drifting Championship alongside the Modified Live show n' shine paddock. It promises to be a great day and of course Speedhunters will be there to cover the action !

Paddy
