Social&gt;&gt; How Are You Speedhunting This Weekend?

June 12, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

We asked on our Facebook and Twitter pages what you were all doing this weekend and we received varied and worldwide response. Check out the responses we that received after the jump.

Kimaru Trackmann: My friends are doin a run with a Toyota Mark 2 and a vits rs in the streets of Nairobi…

Robin Eeckhout: On Saturday there is a rally in Belgium, 12h of Wervik. And on Sunday we are going to takes some pictures @ Kester, its belgian championship MX…

Dieguez Sébastien: I'm heading to Tayac in Gironde (near Saint Emilion) for a grass track race….

Taylor Shull: Eurowerks 4

Megan Sweeney: More drag racing and checking out a shop I may get work done at. Speedhunting is every weekend for me!

Kurtis Goat: DMCC West Round 1 Calgary AB !!!

Oliver Gutperl: Planespotting jets at ILA Berlin Airshow and mentally preparing for the Doelln 5000 on the next weekend (a 5000m autocross on an abandoned Warsaw Pact Nuclear bomber airfield).

Oleg Lozovoy: I'll be speedhunting at Russian Drift Series Round 3, which will take place in Moscow this weekend!

Justin Moore: NFS Most Wanted & Nitto 1320 Legends will be my speedhunting oppurtunities

Chris Hecht: Local car show this Friday and Saturday! Takin' the family!

Hugo Lefrançois: replacing 2 ball joints on my E30, maybe some valve adjustment

Robert Whitworth: skids on private skid pad

Wrecklezz Tuning: Taking my car for a shakedown road tune style (hope it doesn't blow up!)

Kalen Drake: early entries and dirt drops, finding new drift spots, then drinking some beer, then flying to maryland! haha a solid weekend

Philip Douglas Hammar: GT Legends Historic FIA racing on the PC.

James Rann: Maybe installing my 2-way LSD in my AE86. OR maybe just taking it for a run through the hills. That and some gravity powered downhill skateboarding (with drifts)

Alex Buwalda continues: refit on the 3000GT VR4, replace and refurb everything!

Markus Pinn: Planning on selling my beloved e36 and buying an e90.

So how are YOU speedhunting?

-Linhbergh
