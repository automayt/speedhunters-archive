News&gt;&gt;a Repeat In Jersey

June 5, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

It was an extreme case of déjà vu at Wall Speedway this evening as Daijiro Yoshihara once again beat Vaughn Gittin Jr. in the final match – for his second straight victory. Meanwhile Team Falken was on track to repeat their 2009 podium sweep, until Tanner Foust defeated Darren McNamara in a heated battle for third.

Much, much more to come from FD New Jersey, but for now, congrats to Dai, Vaughn, Tanner, and everyone else who brought the moves this weekend.

-Mike Garrett
