Car Spotlight&gt;&gt;samurai 510

June 23, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

As I mentioned in my post yesterday, last weekend's Cars & Coffee event had a large showing of old school Nissans. There were a few Skylines imported from Japan, a couple 240Z's, and a few Datsun 510's – one of which really caught my eye.

It looks like this particular 510 has been built with a mix of tuner car and retro elements, and surprisingly, it works.

I didn't get a chance to look under the hood, but I'm pretty sure it's not stock under there. SR? KA? Hopped up L-series? Even without knowing about the engine setup, I was still drawn to this little Datsun.

I've always thought that 510's with molded fender flares looked a little cheesy, but not so on this car. Not sure exactly how these were done, but they look great.

The fact that it's running a perfect fitting set of 15" Watanabes also helps.

The inside is just as nice as the outside. When was the last time you saw a 510 with an interior this clean?

The unique color choice is another factor that gives the car appeal.

It's always nice to see a popular car like the 510 buitl a unique way, but not to the point of being gimmicky or over the top.

Definitley one of the most interesting examples of the old "five and dime" I've ever seen.

-Mike Garrett
