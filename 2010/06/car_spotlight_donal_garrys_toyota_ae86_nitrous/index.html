Car Spotlight&gt;&gt; Donal Garrys 20v Ae86 With Nitrous

June 8, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

As a foreword, I have to say that up until about twenty four hours before I took this photo, this was one of the cleanest AE86's I had ever layed eyes on. Unfortunately, Donal used the outside wall at Punchestown to re-model the AE86. This however has nothing to do with Donal running out of talent, it's more down to the fact that this guy is one of the most balls out drifters I've ever seen.

This is the same car in 2009, where Donal spent the season harrassing the pro class drivers in the little hachi that could. His driving style earned him the nickname 'Banzai Corolla Guy' and he instantly became a fan favourite.

The car is a two door booted AE86 with a bolt in cage.

14" Work Equips with 185/60's on the front

With Bright Racing 14" and the same tire size on the rear. Liberal amounts of ducktape is a temporary solution to keep the quarter panel in shape.

Inside we have the usual stripped interior with a large tacho.

Every drifters friend : The Hydro Wand.

Donal's friend : A large bottle of Nitrous Oxide.

The 16V 4-AGE has been swapped out for a 20V Blacktop unit. You can see where the nitrous is being injected into the trumpets on the intake.

Back on track, Donal made it to Top 16 before being eliminated by eventual runner up Gavin Lenihan. The fact that this car went from wreck to a full driving and functional car is credit to Donal and to his mates who pulled an all nighter to get the car back on track for the following morning. I'm looking forward to witnessing some more exploits of the 'Banzai Corolla Guy' later in the year …

Paddy
