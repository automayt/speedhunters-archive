In The Moment&gt;&gt; Team Nfs Gt3: Playing In The Sand

June 19, 2010 Jonathan Moore

Or more accurately, playing in the sand and gravel. There are only two hours to go until qualifying for the third round of the European GT3 championship at the Jarama circuit in central Spain. After a great first Free Practice where they posted the eighth fastest time, lead driver Edward Sandstrom in the #76 Team Need For Speed BMW Z4 Coupé attacked the fast downhill Bugatti section too hard, spun and hit the barriers. Now the team are working flat-out to repair the damage and get the car back into working order for the qualifying sessions for tomorrow's races. The clock's ticking… Will they make it?
