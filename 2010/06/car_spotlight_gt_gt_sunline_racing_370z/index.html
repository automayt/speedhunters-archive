Car Spotlight &gt;&gt; Sunline Racing 370z

June 8, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

While at Sunline Racing I couldn't pass up the opportunity to grab some quick shots of one of the latest addition to their demo car fleet, this very aggressive looking Z34 370Z.

The Z is currently under development with new interesting parts being added all the time. To improve engine performance the stock restrictive exhaust system has been replaced with one of those titanium masterpieces Sato-san is so well known for. Together with the remapped ECU the Fairlady not only boast a little more power but if far more responsive and more willing to rev.

The Brembo brakes have been replaced with special SLR-versions of Endless' popular 6-pot calipers, finished off in red like the center bells of the 2-piece rotors. 

The SLR kit includes a carbon fiber front lip spoiler, side skirts and rear bumper skirts as well as a rather large bi-wing carbon spoiler.

This Z34 will be slowly turned into a time attack track car which is why the stock seats have been replaced with Bride fixed bucket seats.

The carbon treatment continues onto the roof, although this is only a cover and not a complete dry-carbon replacement.

I'm sure SLR will continue to extract more performance throughout the course of the year and I really hope to be seeing this car out in action at Time Attack events in Tsukuba and Fuji Speedway.

Sunline Racing USA

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
