News&gt;&gt;masao Suenaga Wins D1gp Rd.2

April 27, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

The second event of the 2010 D1GP season was held over the weekend at Autopolis. Dino had another event, so he couldn't be there to shoot, but it was Masao Suenaga who took the victory after defeating Daigo Saito in the final.

The event also included American drivers Sam Hubinette, Pat Mordaunt, and Mike Pollard, who drove JZZ30 Soarers borrowed from Takahiro Ueno.

Following the win, Suenaga now has a slight lead over M7 teammate Youichi Imamura in the season standings. Round three will be held at Fuji Speedway in early June.

-Mike Garrett
