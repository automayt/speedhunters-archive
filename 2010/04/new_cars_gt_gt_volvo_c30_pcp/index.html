New Cars&gt;&gt; Volvo C30 Pcp

April 21, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Just found this in my inbox. According to Volvo it is still a concept and that it will be revealed at the Göthenborg Carshow. The car will be powered by their 2.5 liter five cylinder engine with a bigger turbo and intercooler. The exterior has been painted in babyblue that contrasts nicely with the black BBS wheels. Will Volvo put this car into production? well we can always pray.

-Jeroen Willemsen
