Random Snap&gt;&gt; Wild Cat Looking For New Home

April 6, 2010 Andy Blackmore

I noticed this sad story over on Crank and Piston the other day. This sad cat is just one (I know of another….) who is being left to die, starved of excitement or action.  It had a troubled birth in Coventry, England, when originally conceived, its power was to come from a 6.2 Litre V12. By the time she entered the world, she had grown into a bit of an overweight ‘moggie’ with a turbocharged V6.

Despite this, this cat was popular and was used on the race track, winning its class at Le Mans before a technical infringement stripped it of a win, and I assume one of its lives.

Sadly, this kitty is left dying in Qatar, allegedly with just 900km on the clock. According to Crank and Piston, the previous owner never cared for him (or is it a her) had bought the car in Dubai and was last seen in Beirut.

More images of this poor kitty over at Crank and Piston.

I have cleared a space in my garage….not sure our dogs would be impressed.

Andy B
