Random Snap&gt;&gt; The Keep Drifting Fun Van

April 21, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

I received an email from the peanut butter connoisseur, Will Roegge, which contained a photo of the newly wrapped KeepDriftingFun VW Westy. And well, it looks just amazing doesn't it? It's covered in robots, dinosaurs, and spaceships. You really can't get any more fun than that, no? So, if you're in the States and you see this whimsical 37 horse power van trotting about, you might want to turn around and follow it. Wherever that van is headed, you know you're heading to witness the nation's best of grassroots level drifting. I do have to admit that the van can use a nice dose of low though….

Be sure to follow their five month journey on the Keep Drifting Fun blog!

-Linhbergh
