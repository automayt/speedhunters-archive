Announcement&gt;&gt; Speedhunters Sticker &amp; Poster Give-away

April 8, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

I've just handed DJ Miki Taka a big stack of new limited edition Speedhunters stickers which she'll be giving away at Formula D Long Beach starting tomorrow. If you spot her in the crowd or find her spinning beats at the Team NFS Electro Lounge she just may give you a set… All you need to do is greet her using the word "m8"… as in "Hiya m8… I am obsessed with rotary powered cars, please give me some Speedhunters stickers"… if she really likes you she may also give you a Speedhunters poster too!
