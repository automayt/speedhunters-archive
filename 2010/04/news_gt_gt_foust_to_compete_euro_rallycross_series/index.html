News&gt;&gt; Foust&#039;s Rallycross Weekend

April 2, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Just a reminder that Formula D, X-Games and Rally America competitor Tanner Foust's making his British Rallycross debut this weekend, at the first round in Lydden Hill,
Kent, UK. We hope to catch up with Tanner at one
of the other events later in the year (he plans to
contest five of the ten rounds of the European Rallycross
Championship this year) to get an inside eye to the
Rallycross world. 

We'll hopefully have coverage of this event early
next week and wish Tanner the best of luck !

Paddy
