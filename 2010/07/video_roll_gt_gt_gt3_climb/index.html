Video Roll &gt;&gt; Gt3 Climb

July 25, 2010 2313281290

In 2009, I worked with Rhys Millen to create a short film, Climb Attack, which documented his Time Attack 2WD class – record setting Pikes Peak run. This year, 2010, I had the honor of working with Jeff Zwart as he campaigned a Porsche GT3 Cup at the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb. Jeff smashed the Time Attack 2WD record set by Rhys in 2009 with a time of 11:31.095. Enjoy the finished short film -GT3 Climb.

- Will Roegge
