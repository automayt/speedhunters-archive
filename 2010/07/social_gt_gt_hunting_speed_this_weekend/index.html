Social&gt;&gt; Hunting Speed This Weekend?

July 2, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

The weekend is now once again upon us. For many of us Americans, it's a weekend filled with friends, family, the grilling of various protein products and the lighting of gun powder. But across the ocean, fellow Speedhunters, Rod Chong, Will Roegge, Paddy McGrath and Jonathan Moore will be documenting the FIA GT races at the gorgeous Paul Ricard race track in the south of France.

Meanwhile, John Brooks will be converging on the Goodwood Festival of Speed in the UK. 

And back on the American west coast, Mike Garrett and I will be celebrating our nation's birthday with a good dosage of desert, sun and tire smoke at this year's All Star Bash!

Since you guys now know how we're spending our weekend, we went ahead and asked on our facebook and twitter pages how you guys were doing your own bit of speedhunting. Here are a few of the responses:

Lea Anne: If it rains, def. slide time with some entry speeds that are of the quickness. ♥

Will Epps: I've been struggling to top 70mph in my Peugeot – Rapid!

Stefan Brencher: Take a trip to the Alps in Austria for downhill racing… BMW ringtool is ready for action!

Jon Sylvester: Shooting some drifting at DMCC west this weekend up in Canada!

Irwin Matta: Attending a massive meet and cruise this Sunday organized by Sydney Car Scene and RexNuts.

Trevor Sheldrake: I hunted Speed a bit too much at Time Attack last Sunday, this weekend I'll be checking the engine for a possible rebuild!

Chaydon Ford: Off to Goodwood Festival of Speed today! Amped!

Nils Plovie: Spa summer classic. Race weekend for vintage race cars and a bit driving around.

Daniel Hollands: pulling rear subframe out of the S13 in prep for stadium drift in a month or so.

Ben Comana: Been testing a Formula Student car all week, weekend is for rest.

David Hornburg: Cruising around town in the S30. Hopefully adjusting the e-brake cable, too.

Edgar Ferman: All Star Bash X

Geoffrey Tyndall: I hunt it with my right foot.

 

So, how're you hunting speed this weekend? Let us know!

-Linhbergh
