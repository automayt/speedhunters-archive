Random Snap&gt;&gt; Radioshack Gtr

July 9, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Things are a bit hectic in Holland right now. It has taken us 32 years but we reached the finals in the World Cup after beating Uruguay on Tuesady. This is a once in a lifetime opportunity. We meet with Spain on Sunday and I really hope we are going to party on Tuesday when we will pay homage to the new World Champions in Amsterdam with an expected 1 million visitors.

But that's not it! The Tour de France has also started and champion, Lance Armstrong, is currently riding for team Radioshack. And tha's how I came across this image. 

This GTR has to be the lowest one I have ever seen.

-Jeroen Willemsen
