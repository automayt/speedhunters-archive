Readers Rides&gt;&gt;legnum Vr-4

November 30, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

A few of you mentioned that I forgot the Mitsubishi Legnum VR-4 when I did my station wagon retrospective yesterday.

It just so happens that Cameron from Sunshine Coast, Australia sent us this great photo of his Legnum. He's out fitted the twin turbo V6 wagon with a Vouge aero kit, Ganador mirrors, Recaro Seats, A Greddy FMIC, and Enkei wheels just to name a few things.

Just another one of those great hot rod wagons from Japan.

-Mike Garrett
