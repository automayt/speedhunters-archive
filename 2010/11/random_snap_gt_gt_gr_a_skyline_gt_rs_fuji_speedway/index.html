Random Snap&gt;&gt; Gr.a Skyline Gt-rs @ Fuji Speedway

November 25, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

What better way is there to spend a national holiday than at the track putting your pride and joy through its paces. Fellow GT-R owner and friend, Akihiro Itoh, was at Fuji Speedway on Tuesday with the guys from the Skyline Sports Club Tokyo (SSCT) and spotted this nice line up of replica GR.A BNR32s in the paddock. Not a bad collection! Looking at this shot reminded me that the Nismo Festival isn't too far off now!
