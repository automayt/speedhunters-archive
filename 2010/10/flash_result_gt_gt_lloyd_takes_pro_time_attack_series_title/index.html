Flash Result&gt;&gt; Lloyd Takes Pro Time Attack Series Title

October 11, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

With the sun setting on the Norfolk based Snetterton circuit, Gareth Lloyd has taken the 2010 Pro class Time Attack Series championship title. Coming into this final round, it was possible for four drivers to clinch the crown in pro class, but everyone's eyes were on Gareth Lloyd in the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution and Steve Guglielmi in the VAG powered Lotus Elise. The event and indeed the championship came down to the last laps of 2010 but it was Lloyd who emerged champion. I'll have full coverage and results from the rest of the championship over the coming days.
