Social&gt;&gt;we Want Your Drag Cars!

October 5, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Do you own a super cool drag car, or know somebody that does? We want to see it.

We are looking for Reader-owned drag cars of all types. In fact it doesn't even have to be cars. Got shots of a cool local drag strip or something else drag related? Why not?

We can't have a successful drag month without some reader precipitation, so send a couple photos (sized around 672×448 px) and some info to SpeedhuntersMike(at)gmail.com

Looking forward to seeing what you send in!

-Mike Garrett
