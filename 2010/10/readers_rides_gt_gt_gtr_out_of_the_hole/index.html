Readers Rides&gt;&gt;gtr Out Of The Hole

October 21, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Quite a few drag Skylines posted here recently eh? Here's one more – a street car owned by Mark Riccioni from the UK. You might remember Mark's guest blog on his previous FD RX7 project. His latest project is this R32 GTR with a single turbo conversion and a dyno-proven 580 wheel horsepower.

After running a 10.90 on the car's third pass down the strip, Mark is looking to get his ET's nto the low 10, high 9 second range – all while remaining totally streetable.

Awesome.

-Mike Garrett
