Random Snap&gt;&gt; Mad Mike In Nz

October 25, 2010 Matt Malcolm

"It's been a while" said Mike as we quickly exchanged words over the angry laugh of his 500hp, quad rotor Mazda RX-7. I swear if I could put the noise his car makes into a drink I would never leave the bar! Hearing and seeing it back in New Zealand was purely intoxicating.
