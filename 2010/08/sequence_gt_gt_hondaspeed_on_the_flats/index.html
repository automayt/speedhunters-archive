Sequence&gt;&gt; Hondaspeed On The Flats

August 25, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

One of the last things I shot at Bonneville was this sequence of the Hondata RSX launching down the salt. As most of the teams had left the event by this time, I was able to stand immediately behind the car and shoot this series of photos.

This got me thinking that we could introduce a new type of Speedhunters story called "SEQUENCE"

It's a series of action shots that help to show you a sequence of events.

In this case we are looking at the Hondata RSX attacking the Bonneville Course.

And it starts to disappear into a mirage effect…

Which makes the car look like its floating in the air… an unusual optical illusion.

I pulled up the speed data for this run off the SCTA website and it looks like so:

Qtr: 183.604 mph

Mile1: 186.322 mph

Mile2: 186.944 mph

Mile3: 166.095 mph

Exit: 103.872 mph
