News&gt;&gt; The G1-gp

August 3, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Just got word from Andy over at Powervehicles.com that the upcoming second round of the G1 Grand Prix is set to have close to 30 people participating in the event. It’s really incredible how much this international competition has grown since its inaugural round during the Autumn Drift Matsuri last year. As ever, Team Orange drivers Nobushige Kumakubo and Naoto Suenaga will be the judges at the event, which will be held at the Nishi Short Course on the 20th of this month, the day before the Summer Drift Matsuri kicks off. Of course I’ll be there to cover the action and enjoy the tasty BBQ that will follow!

Check out Andy’s blog for more info on the event and how one can join.

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
