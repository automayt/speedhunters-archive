Trax Feature This Terms And Conditions

August 15, 2010 Rod Chong

Terms and Conditions

1. General

The opening time and date of the contest is Sunday 2nd September 2012 at 09:00 GMT, and the closing date and time for receipt of entries is Sunday 2nd September 2012 at 15:30 GMT. Entries submitted after this time will not be considered.

2. Restrictions

This competition is open to UK residents aged 17 and over only. Employees (and members of their immediate family) of ‘Electronic Arts’ (‘EA’) (‘Promoter’), their agents and anyone connected to this competition are not eligible to enter.

3. Rules to participate

To enter, attendees of the Trax show at Silverstone Circuit are to upload a minimum of one (1) photo of their car on Twitter and/or Instagram which must be hashtagged as #FEATURETHIS.  Only one car can be entered per person. Photos are to be taken with a camera phone and must be taken on the day of the competion at the Trax Silverstone on Sunday 2nd September 2012.

During the show the top 250 entrants will be messaged on Instagram and/or Twitter to each collect oneSpeedhunters sticker from the Speedhunters’ onsite booth. Photos will be judged by a Speedhunters’ staff member against the following criteria; 50% car build quality, 25% car build originality and 25% car model type originality.

Out of the 250 top entrants ten (10) cars will be shortlisted and will be judged onsite at the event by the Speedhunters photography team against the criteria above. One (1) final winner will be chosen from the top ten (10).

No purchase of any goods or services is necessary to be eligible for entry into this competition.

The judges’ decision is final. No correspondence will be entered into.

4. Prize(s)

There will be one (1) overall winner who will receive one (1) photography shoot of their car by Bryn Musselwhite, which will then appear as a full feature post in Speedhunters. The photography session will take between two (2) and four (4) hours. The feature post will include the photographs from the shoot and an article about the car build, and will be published on Speedhunters.com before Friday 30th November 2012.

In order to receive the prize the winner will need to make their car available for one (1) weekend day at a mutually agreed UK location with the Speedhunters’ photographer before the end of October 2012.

The winner will be required to sign an Assignment of Rights.

There are no alternatives to these prizes. They are as stated and are non-transferable. If due to circumstances beyond the control of EA, EA is unable to provide the stated prizes, EA reserves the right to award substitute prizes of equal value.  All prizes are subject to the terms and conditions of the supplier.

5. Results

The winner will be directly messaged over Instagram and Twitter with their name also called out over the PA system on Sunday 2nd September 2012 at 16.00 GMT.

If the winner of the prize cannot be traced after reasonable efforts have been made by EA, EA will be entitled to select alternative winner from among the entrants.

A full list of who won will be available for four (4) weeks following the day of the selection of winners. This list can be obtained by writing to EA Easy, Västgötagatan 2, 118 27 Stockholm, Sweden.

If proof of age and/or parental consent is requested, and if satisfactory proof of age and/or parental consent is not received, EA reserves the rights to choose another winner.
