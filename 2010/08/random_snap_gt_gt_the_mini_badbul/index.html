Random Snap&gt;&gt;the Mini Badbul

August 17, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Speedhunters reader and Mad Mike fan Heri sent us this picture of his RC replica of the Bad Bul.

Based on a TA05 with a Square drift conversion, Heri outfitted the car Speedway Pal wheels, a custom interior, and custom Red Bull graphics.

Great work Heri! Hopefully we'll see a replica of the Team NFS version next!

-Mike Garrett
