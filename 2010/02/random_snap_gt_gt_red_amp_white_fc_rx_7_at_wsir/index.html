Random Snap&gt;&gt; Red &amp; White Fc Rx-7 At Wsir

February 24, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

This past weekend was quite the automotive odyssey. I spent two days at Willow Springs International Raceway and a day at Buttonwillow Racetrack for Hellaflush 3.5. While at Willow Springs on Saturday, I spotted this very clean example of a drift FC RX-7 at an event on the balcony skid pad. Who knows how long it'll last being that clean, but the deep red + white Enkei RPF-1 wheels is a classic color combination and just looks fantastic!

Coverage from Hellaflush 3.5 at Buttonwillow is coming very soon. So keep an eye out for it!

-Linhbergh
