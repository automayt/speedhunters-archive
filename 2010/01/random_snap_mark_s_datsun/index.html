Random Snap&gt;&gt;mark&#039;s Datsun

January 18, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Thought I'd follow up the gasser Plymouth with another slightly lower wagon of the same vintage built on the other side of the world. This '67 Datsun 411SSS is Mark Arcenal's latest project, and if it's anything like his other projects it should be one hell of a car when it's done.

The vintage Datsun is under construction right now at the Bay Area's DGR Fabrication.

Check Mark's blog for a few more photos from the early stages of the build.

-Mike Garrett
