Car Life&gt;&gt; Lost In London - Pt.ii

September 23, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Picking up where I left off yesterday, we continued south (I think) still along Park Lane. Next up was this fetching F430 Spyder sitting somewhere nearish Buckingham Palace. I always preferred the F430 over the F360 but having recently seen two F458s at seperate shows, I think the newest Ferrari has stolen my heart. The previous cars (360 & 430) look almost, I don't know, ordinary beside the 458. It really is an incredible looking car.

This GT3 looked absolutely menacing in black with black with black wheels.

Whilst this Exige looked a little out of place amongst the regular traffic.

Whilst maybe not entirely exotic, the E46 M3 still looks (and sounds) like all good M series cars should.

The Fiat 500 Abarth was probably the most realistic of all the cars we spotted.

Not a bad company car …

I was unaware that a new Range Rover had been released. Apparently it's called the 'ParkWhereEverYouDamnWellLike'

I love the sound of V10 in the evening …

DB9 Volante. I still don't think I'd ever drive a soft top though (Not like I'd ever a DB9 down but still)

Now this is a pretty car, the new Ferrari California.

Across the road was this GT3 RS.

Just another one of those 599 GTB's …

Except this one had a rather interesting birthmark …

I think the C63 AMG looks so much better in a darker colour.

After a bit of a detour, we finally arrived at Harrods where this SLS was awaiting it's owner.

I'm unsure about the new SLS, I just don't think it'll be held in the same esteem as the 300SL. What do you think ?

This dark purple R8 on black wheels looked sinister. The Quattroporte behind it wasn't too bad either.

A GT Continental in the wild …

The GT3 RS from the first post on a run to the shops. Must be a tough life !

Paddy
