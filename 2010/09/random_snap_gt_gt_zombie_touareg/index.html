Random Snap&gt;&gt; Zombie Touareg?

September 22, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

After the controversy over the Zombie Golf we featured earlier in the month, one of our readers, TJ, submitted this image he found of what looks like a Zombie Touareg. After some investigation, it looks like this was one of the cars that starred in the new RepoMen film alongside Jude Law and Forest Whitaker. I'll have to concede that it actually looks pretty awesome, I wonder if I can get one as Speedhunters-mobile ?
