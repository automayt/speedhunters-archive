Social&gt;&gt;we Need A New Motto

September 27, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

If you look up at the top right corner of your screen, under the main Speedhunters logo you'll see the phrase "Car Culture at Large".

While that phrase definitely still holds true to the site, we thought it was time for a change. But what should we replace it with?

That's where you guys come in.

We thought it would be best to take suggestions from our readers as to what our new motto/catchphrase should be. It's pretty simple. Post your suggestions in the comments section, and we'll go through and post our favorites in a final poll to decide the winner.

Obviously, it shouldn't be longer than once sentence, but other than that you're free to run wild. Most likely the winner will wind up with some sort of Speedhunters swag.

Alright then, let's hear them!

-Mike Garrett
