Random Snap &gt;&gt; Into The Garage

September 26, 2010 Len Clarke

Missing SUPER GT coverage? Me too! With the cancellation of the Fuji round a couple of weeks back* there's a gaping hole in the latter part of the season. So to fill the void, I've decided to post a few random snaps of action from earlier this season. This image is of satoshi Motoyama being pushed back into the garage at Sepang, shortly before putting in a sublime qualifying performance to take third on the grid. 

*Round 7 was cancelled due to landslides caused by a nasty typhoon that saw entire roads around Fuji Speedway taken out – as well as a host of other damage to the surrounding area.  No replacement event has been announced. The final round of the season takes place at Twin Ring Motegi on October 23-24. 

 – Len Clarke
