Random Snap&gt;&gt; Mike Burroughs&#039; Reborn E36

May 22, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Mike Burroughs' is a fellow photographer who's work I dearly admire on a normal basis. He also, in my opinion, is one of the best at fixing up BMWs. This is his recently unveiled and reborn E36 with an S54 laying under the hood. The highlight of this car, other than it's exterior looks are the beautiful trumpets that sit in the engine bay. Check the trumpets in this well done video featuring the car of Mike talking about the car here. You can also check out a few more of Mike's photos of the car and a more information of this car on this StanceWorks forum thread.

I highly recommend watching the video first.

-Linhbergh
