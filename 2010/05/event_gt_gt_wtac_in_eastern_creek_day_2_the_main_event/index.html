Event&gt;&gt; Wtac - The Main Event

May 22, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Anything could have happened today at the World Time Attack Challenge in Eastern Creek Raceway. The top cars were very evenly matched but it was the Cyber Evo/Garage HRS team that came away victorious. After spending most of yesterday fitting a new engine they were back in top form this morning allowing Tarzan to get to business right away with a fastest lap of 1:30:587.

Tarzan was a very happy man!!

It was great to see that two out of the four Japanese teams that made it to the event were on the podium…

…the Sierra Sierra driven by David Empringham grabbing second spot…

…with the Tomei/Cusco/Voltex Impreza coming in third position, just 0.017 sec behind! 

Of course it was Tarzan Yamada behind the wheel of the blue/white/red Impreza, which meant he is bringing home two trophies!

The Panspeed RX-7 finished in a respectable fifth position behind the GT3-spec Prep'd Motorsport Elise.

The Panspeed car was rocking strategically placed Speedhunters stickers! Thanks guys!

Mark Berry in the Advan/Hi Octane Racing R34 GT-R managed to come in 6th position with a 1:34:109…

…with the team satisfied with the performance of the car against the top level Time Attack machines.

Kinoshita, at the wheel of the R-Magic RX-7, was looking all set to pin down a real fast time with the car…

…after his mechanics sorted out all the little gremlins they had the previous day, including convincing the scrutineer that a fuel tank in the passenger seat is great for optimal weight balance.

Despite that, the gearbox ended up failing, putting an end to R-Magic's hopes. Here is the car cruising back towards the pits feeling sorry for itself.

The Cyber Evo could have probably gone faster than its track-record-breaking time (previously set in 1999 by Mark Skaife's V8 Supercar – running on slicks)…

…but right as Tarzan was going for one more attempt the massive rear Voltex wing literally ripped off the CFRP trunk, bringing most of the top section with it. This occurred because the wing was directly bolted onto the trunk, not onto a self-supporting frame directly connected to the chassis. 

Eventually the spoiler, along with the pice of the C-West trunk, made it back to the pits! I'm sure the Cyber Evo and Garage HRS team learned a lesson today!

Despite the unexpected mishap however, Tarzan already knew he had pretty much won. Here he is looking very pleased in the Tomei pit.

It's been a very exciting and busy three days at Eastern Creek. I will be taking a deeper look at the event, especially all the other cars that participated in the other classes, not to mention the usual walk through the paddock and pits. Make sure you don't miss the rest of our Australian Speedhunting coverage as there is a lot more to come.

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
