Random Snap&gt;&gt; Alfa Romeo 155 V6 Ti

May 14, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

I missed the event Techno Classica Event in Essen, so after seeing pictures of that show on the Internet I promised myself I won't miss it next year. I still wanted to dedicate a random snap, but I was in doubt which car would get the honor. In the end this Alfa Romeo 155 V6 Ti got my vote.

Alfa Corsa campaigned the car in the 1993 DTM season and they did it with great success. With Nicola Larini behind the wheels they dominated the 1993 season with 11 wins in 22 races. Although it had a short life, 1996 was their last season,  it was a great success for both Alfa Corsa and Alfa Romeo.

-Jeroen Willemsen
