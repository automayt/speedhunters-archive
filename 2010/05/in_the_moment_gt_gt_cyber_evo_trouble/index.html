In The Moment&gt;&gt; Cyber Evo Trouble

May 20, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Things are not looking too good for the Cyber Evo/Garage HRS guys. After snapping a cam during yesterday's session they have spent the whole day working on the engine. Last time Rod dropped by the garage the head was completely off the engine, so things are not looking too good. With rain on the forecast for tomorrow this afternoon's final session may be the last chance everyone will have to get the best times. The High Octane R34 is in the same boat, some problems not allowing them to go out for a full-on lap yet. Stay tuned for more from Eastern Creek.

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
