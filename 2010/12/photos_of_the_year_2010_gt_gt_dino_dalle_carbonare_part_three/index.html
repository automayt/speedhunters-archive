Photos Of The Year 2010&gt;&gt; Dino Dalle Carbonare Part Three

December 30, 2010 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Right, let's continue with my look at my favorite pictures of the year and pick up from where I left off in Part Two:

Above – D1 Round 5, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Hibino getting four wheels in the air taking full advantage of the new line the organizers opened up

R's Meeting 2010, Fuji Speedway, Shizuoka-ken – A line up of BNR34s

AE86 Meeting, Daikoku Parking Area, Yokohama – Panda perfection

Izu Skyline, Izu Peninsula, Shizuoka-ken – A memorable drive with friends. Apart from bake fade my car felt superb around the challenging roads.

Izu Skyline, Izu Peninsula, Shizuoka-ken – My buddy Aki and his super clean R33 GT-R. Eventually when his car will be completed I will shoot it properly. The question is, will it ever be finished?

Izu Skyline, Izu Peninsula, Shizuoka-ken – One of the nicest I've ever come across. Simply stunningly beautiful 

Rocky Auto, Okazaki, Aichi-ken – S20 powered Hakosuka

D1 Round 5, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Tokita landing his big Crown hard at Minami course

D1 Round 5, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Saito getting some serious air

D1 Round 5, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken - Nomura struggling to stand up after his massive head-on crash. One of the biggest accidents I've ever witnessed in D1

Summer Drift Matsuri, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Sideways exciting the hairpin at Higashi

Summer Drift Matsuri, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – A special spectator 

Summer Drift Matsuri, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Suenaga getting his practice RPS13 crossed up through the Nishi-course S-bend

Toyota Press Event, Fuji Speedway, Shizuoka-ken - A ride along in the LFA Nurbugring race car with Kinoshita at the wheel. One memorable experience!

Toyota Press Event, Fuji Speedway, Shizuoka-ken – G's Mark X, Toyota is on to a winner with this car. When we will see it out on the street is still not known.

Summer Drift Matsuri, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – Chris DeJager getting ready to go out on track

Chiba Port, Chiba, Chiba-ken – A Newera Imports built FD3S ready to be loaded onto a ship

Chiba Port, Chiba, Chiba-ken – Another Newera special, now getting completely rebuilt and transformed 

Phoenix's Power, Kyoto – A 600+HP customer R35 GT-R

Somewhere along the Tohoku Expressway - Yet another memorable drive, this time in an Audi R8 V10

Summer Drift Matsuri, Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima-ken – A disappearing act

I've got one final selection of pictures to come!  Back soon with more.

- Dino Dalle Carbonare
