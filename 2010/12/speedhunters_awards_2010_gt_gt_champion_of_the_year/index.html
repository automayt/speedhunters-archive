Speedhunters Awards 2010&gt;&gt; Champion Of The Year

December 28, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

A year is a long time to fight for something. But to stand on the top of that podium as the best in your motorsport discipline is a fight which results in the champagne tasting so much sweeter than it ever has. It's time to choose which one of these champions is the best of them all. 

Formula One World Championship: Sebastian Vettel

World Rally Championship: Sebastien Loeb

FIA GT1 World Championship: Andrea Bertolini / Michael Bartels

World Touring Car Championship: Yvan Muller

NASCAR Sprint Cup: Jimmie Johnson

DTM: Paul De Resta

Dakar Rally: Carlos Sainz / Lucas Cruz

24 Hours of Nürburgring: Jörg Müller / Augusto Farfus / Uwe Alzen / Pedro Lamy

Formula Drift: Vaughn Gittin Jr.

D1GP: Youichi Imamura

24 Hours of Le Mans LMP1: Mike Rockenfeller / Timo Bernhard / Romain Dumas

Race of Champions: Filipe Albuquerque

World Time Attack Challenge: Eiji "Tarzan" Yamada

Tsukuba Super Lap Battle: Kikuchi Yasushi

Time Attack Series Pro Champion: Gareth Lloyd

NHRA Top Fuel: Larry Dixon

NHRA Funny Car: John Force

NHRA Pro Stock: Gred Anderson

NHRA Pro Mod: Von Smith

JDM All Stars: Luke Fink

FIA European GT3 Championship: Marco Holzer / Paul van Splunteren

24 Hours of Daytona: João Barbosa / Terry Borcheller / Ryan Dalziel / Mike Rockenfeller

Le Mans Series: Stéphane Sarrazin

American Le Mans Series LMP: Simone Pagenaud / David Brabham

American Le Mans Series GT2: Patrick Long / Jörg Bergmeister

IndyCar Series: Dario Franchitti

British Touring Car Championship: Jason Plato

Australian Supercar V8 Championship: James Courtney

Super GT GT-500: Takashi Kogure / Loïc Duval

Super GT GT-300: Kazuki Hoshino / Masataka Yanagida

NASCAR Nationwide: Brad Keselowski

GP2: Pastor Maldonado

Formula Nippon: João Paulo de Oliveira

Redline Time Attack Super Modified AWD: Jeff Westphal

Stock Car Brasil: Max Wilson

Superlap Battle: David Empringham

Pro Drift Ireland: James Deane

X Games: Tanner Foust

IRC: Juho Hänninen / Mikko Markkula

Rally America: Antoine L'Estage

TC2000: Norberto Fontana

So who is the best of the best then? Leave a comment with your choice.
