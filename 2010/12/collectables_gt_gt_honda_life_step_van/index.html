Collectables&gt;&gt;honda Life Step Van

December 28, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

Just a couple days left in Collectables month, but the cool reader material keeps popping up. Christian from Germany sent us this photo of his 1/20 scale Aoshima Honda Life Step Van.

Christian added some personality to the little micro van by slamming it, adding some 1/24 scale Rays Garcia Tune Choppy wheels, and some other accessories.

Whether scale or full-size, it's always cool to see unique "builds" like this.

-Mike Garrett
