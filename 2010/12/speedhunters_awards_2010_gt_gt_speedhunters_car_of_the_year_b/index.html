Speedhunters Awards 2010&gt;&gt; Vote For The Car Of The Year

December 29, 2010 Charles Kha

This is the big one: who deserves to be crowned the 2010 Speedhunters Car of the Year? This new award pits the winners of each category against one another, irrespective of their genre or discipline. Each nominee was chosen by you in our month-long voting frenzy. It's certainly a diverse selection of cars and we honestly have no idea which car you'll vote to be the inaugural Speedhunters Car of the Year!

Ford Focus RS500 – Best New Car winner

Sumo Power R35 GT-R – Race Car of the Year winner

Team Need for Speed RTR-X – Demo Car of the Year winner

Rauh-Welt 'Bordeaux' Porsche 993 – Street Car of the Year winner

RWB 'West Holly Wood' Porsche 964 – Retro Machine of the Year joint-winner

Henry’s S12 – Retro Machine of the Year joint-winner

Drift Union’s Subaru Legacy – Street Drift Car of the Year

Mad Mike Whiddett’s Mazda RX-8 – Pro Drift Car of the Year

 

Antoine de Cardaillac’s 280Z – Reader’s Car of the Year winner

Mike Burrough’s Rusty BMW – Rat of the Year winner

 

Porsche 918 – Concept Car of the Year winner

This is going to be fascinating!

- Charles Kha
