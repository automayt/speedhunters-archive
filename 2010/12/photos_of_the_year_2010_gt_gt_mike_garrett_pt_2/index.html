Photos Of The Year 2010&gt;&gt;mike Garrett Pt.2

December 8, 2010 TheSpeedhunters

For the second part of my Photos of the Year for 2010, I'll keep the
chronological theme going. Here are some of my favorite personal shots
from May through July.

Above-

Formula D Rd.3, Wall Speedway, Wall Township, New Jersey. JTP rides the wall in the Garden State.

Fresno Dragway Reunion, Eagle Field California. Gassers in the dust. This was one of the coolest events I shot this year for sure.

All Star Bash X, Willow Springs International Raceway, California – Benson Hsu at All Star Bash. Love how this one came up.

Steen Chassis, Signal Hill, California. The beginning of the RTR-X!

Somewhere in Central California – Great times cruising back from the Eagle Field Drags in a '69 Hurst/Olds and a '70 GTO.

Formula D Rd.2, Road Atlanta, Braselton, GA. – From a photographer's perspective, Road Atlanta has to be one of the best tracks on the FD schedule.

Targa Trophy, Hollywood, California. A pair of Ferraris rest before heading to San Diego on the Targa Trophy road rally.

Eibach Honda Meet, Corona, California. My first time at the Honda Eibach meet. "Huge" doesn't even begin to describe this gathering of Honda fanatics.

VW Classic, Irvine California. Cal Look VW's on display at the annual Orange County VW Classic.

Formula D Rd.2, Road Atlanta, Braselton, GA. Chris Forsberg slides through the Georgia night.

Cars & Coffee, Irvine, California. Just another early Saturday morning in Irvine.

Goodguys OC Get Together, Costa Mesa, California. Muscle cars attack the cones at the Goodguys Autocross.

Fatlace One of One, Road Atlanta, Braselton, GA. Lexus SC slammage at the Fatlace FD Atlanta car show.

Dragfest, Famoso Raceway California.  Another one of this year's great hot rod events, Dragfest at Famoso Raceway.

Gas Station, Clovis, California. Had to include a gas station photo in here. My friend Mike's Datsun 510 before a night of cruising.

All Star Bash X, Willow Springs International Raceway, California. Another shot from ASB X, John Rusakoff's AE86 street car.

Mooneyes Summer Show, Irwindale Speedway, California. Custom paintwork shines in the blazing hot sun at Irwindale.

Formula D Rd.4, Evergreen Speedway, Monroe, Washington. Patrick Mordaunt and Fredric Aasbo come through the bank during practice at FD Rd.4.

Mooneyes Summer Show, Irwindale Speedway, Irwindale, California.  Pinstriper's heaven.

Formula D Rd.3, Wall Speedway, Wall Township, New Jersey. Brostangs forever!

To be continued…

-Mike Garrett
