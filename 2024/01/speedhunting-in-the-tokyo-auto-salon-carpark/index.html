Speedhunting In The Tokyo Auto Salon Carpark

January 19, 2024 TheSpeedhunters

As we’ve seen over the course of the last week, there are many sides to the Tokyo Auto Salon. And we’re not just talking about the giant annual tuning event itself, but also the other gatherings held around Japan’s capital city that coincide with it.

One of our favorite ‘events’ outside of TAS is literally outside TAS – the Makuhari Messe carpark. Of course, this isn’t actually an event, but given what you can find parked up at any time during all three days of Tokyo Auto Salon, it might as well be.

After spending Friday and Saturday shooting all the cars inside for our huge TAS gallery post this year, Rick returned on Sunday and took a quick poke around the carpark before making his way back into the show. Enjoy the gallery below of what he found…
