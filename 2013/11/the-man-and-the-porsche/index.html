The Man &amp; The Porsche

November 26, 2013 Mike Garrett

Spa and a Porsche RSR. Does it get much better than that? It does if that RSR happens to be equipped with a pair of turbochargers. This is the Bi-Turbo 911 RSR from Dijkstra Racing Team competing at Spa’s Supercar Challenge, as captured by Clash Production.

Clash produced a series of videos showing the unusual 700 horsepower 911 during the build-up to the race and then dicing through traffic after the green flag drops.

Perhaps you’d like to leave the cinematics behind and just enjoy the onboard footage of the turbocharged beast passing other cars like they are standing still.

Remind anyone else of a video game?
