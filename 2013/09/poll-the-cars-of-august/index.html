Poll: The Cars Of August

September 5, 2013 Mike Garrett

As hard as it might be to believe, it’s already September, and for most people around the world a sign that fall is on its way. As we roll through the ninth month of the year it’s time to take one last look back at August with our monthly feature car showdown. August saw a number of impressive machines pop up on the pages of Speedhunters ranging from wide-bodied BMWs to traditional customs, time attackers and rare competition cars.

Now we need you guys to check out the list below, and then choose your favorite.

Here they are:

Sedan on the Salt: A Mercury Kustom

Kyusha Dreams: Rocky Auto’s Carbon Hakosuka

Carbon Not Dated: The Secret Zakspeed Calibra

Step-By-Step: Inside a Time-Attacking 650hp Silvia

The Best of the Best: Global Auto’s Skyline GT-R

Wild Child: Aston Martin V8 Vantage Zagato

The Vantage Advantage: Aston Martin’s GT3 Racer

A Century in the Making: The Aston Martin CC100

Not Played Out: A West Coast VW Done Right

Bold Statements: Liberty Walk M3

German Muscle: Weistec’s CLK63 AMG Black

BTM Continuation Cheetah: Up Close and Personal

Highway to the Danger Zone: The Tomcat S13
