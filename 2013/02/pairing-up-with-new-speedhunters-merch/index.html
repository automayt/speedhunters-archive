One New Garm &amp; A Thing To Hang Round Your Neck

February 20, 2013 TheSpeedhunters

We’re very pleased to announce the latest pair of additions to the Speedhunters collection. First up, modelled here by the one and only MadMike, is the camel-shade T featuring the #Maximum Attack logo on the front…

…and some tidy Speedhunters type on the shoulder. The Japanese text is pronounced “Zenkai” which means “Keep Open”. It’s Japanese drift terminology for keeping your foot planted solid on the throttle.

The other new piece is this super bold red lanyard packed full of Speedhunters logos.

Both are available right now in the Speedhunters store, so head over there to snap them up before they disappear –  the original Cranberry coloured #Maximum Attack shirt has already sold out in the US!

-The Speedhunters Team
