Poll: The Cars Of June

July 5, 2013 Paddy McGrath

I don’t think we’re making it easy for you this month. From Rotiform’s exquisite 964 to that Lancia, we spent the month of June showcasing world exclusives of some of the best builds on the planet right now. One aspect of the job I really enjoy is collating these feature cars, as it either reminds me of some of these features or I realise that there’s one or two that may have slipped past me during the month. Before we kick off the voting, last month’s winner – by quite a narrow margin – was the Porsche-powered VW Bus.
