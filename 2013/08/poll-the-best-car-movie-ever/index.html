Poll: The Best Car Movie Ever

August 9, 2013 Mike Garrett

Last week, we had a little (or actually a huge) discussion about the greatest car movies of all time. Now that you guys have had a chance to share your thoughts on the matter, we figured it was only right to put together a proper poll to answer the question for once and for all. We’ve gone through and picked 20 of the most commonly suggested films from last week’s post and assembled them below into a straightforward poll. All you have to do is go through it and choose the best. Make your vote and be heard!

Furthermore, if there are any movies on the list that you haven’t seen before, now could be the right time to do so.
