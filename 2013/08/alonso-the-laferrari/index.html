Alonso &amp; The Laferrari

August 8, 2013 Mike Garrett

As Ferrari continues final development of the stunning LaFerrari , it recently handed over the keys to F1 ace Fernando Alonso to get some of his feedback on the progress. Needless to say, a film crew was on-hand to record it for the world to see. Of course, this isn’t the first time Alonso has jumped onboard the project, as both he and teammate Felipe Massa have been involved in the car’s development since the very beginning. Part of me wonders how Fernando can drive such a car without laughing and having a massive grin on his face, but I guess being an F1 pilot will do that to you.
