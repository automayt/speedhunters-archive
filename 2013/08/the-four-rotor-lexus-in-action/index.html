The Four-rotor Lexus In Action

August 30, 2013 Mike Garrett

Earlier this year we brought you guys a full a feature on an incredible four-rotor powered Lexus GS out of New Zealand, which I’m sure you remember well. If there was ever a car that needed to be witnessed and heard in motion, this would be it. In this freshly released video from the NZ Drift Nationals, we finally get to see the beast in action. There’s really not much to say other than it’s just as awesome as you’d expect.
