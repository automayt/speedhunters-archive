Motorhead: Bugatti Veyron Fuji Attack

April 16, 2013 Dino Dalle Carbonare

MotorHead is working hard at building an online presence to compliment its amazing magazine, so for every main feature the team put together in print, they back it up with an online film to really bring the story to life. For Volume 07 MotorHead came up with the idea to take a prohibitively-expensive Bugatti Veyron Grand Sport to Fuji Speedway and let pro driver Seiji Ara loose in it. Hit play and enjoy this rather special time attack session, then check back next week when we take our own look at the very same car!
