Discussion: Formula Drift 2013 Predictions

April 12, 2013 Mike Garrett

As you are reading this, the 2013 Formula Drift season is officially underway on the streets of Long Beach. While some of you are lucky enough to be at the event this weekend, many more will be following the action live on the Driftstream from across the world.

I figured now would be a good time to have a fun chat and ask for your predictions on the 2013 season. Can Daigo Saito retain his title? Will a new driver rise to the top? Who will be the top rookie? Which new car will shine on the track? Any other big predictions for the year?

Let’s discuss!

-Mike
