Poll: The Cars Of May

June 11, 2013 Paddy McGrath

Better late than never, here’s our round up of all the cars featured during the month of May. As per usual, it’s an eclectic mix of machines from all around the world. From Sweden’s Hemizon to New Zealand’s MGAWOT II, I think that we have again delivered an unrivalled variety of content.

Last month’s poll was a close affair, with the 1JZ Powered Caddy pipping the McLaren F1 LM by a meagre 78 votes. How close will it be this month? Be sure to check out all of the options before casting your vote. As always, don’t be shy in telling us why you leant one way over the other.
