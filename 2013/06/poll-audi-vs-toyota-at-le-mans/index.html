Poll: Audi Vs. Toyota At Le Mans

June 21, 2013 Paddy McGrath

We’re hours away from race start at Le Mans and all eyes are on the inevitable battle between Audi and Toyota for this year’s outright winner. Toyota showed plenty of promise during their debut at last year’s race, and even realised their potential with wins towards the end of last year’s WEC. Audi have been working hard during the off season and will no doubt start the race as favourites. Whatever happens, I’m sure we’ll be treated to an incredible race but the question on everyone’s mind is: will there be an upset at Le Mans in 2013?

Poll is set to close one minute before race start, so be sure to get your vote in on time.
