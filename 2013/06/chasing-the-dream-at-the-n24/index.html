Chasing The Dream At The N24

June 4, 2013 Jonathan Moore

This year’s Nürburgring 24 Hours was a travail for all involved: glorious and tough in equal measures, just as any race at the Nordschleife should be.

German team Stereoscreen have put together this classy mini documentary, which takes all the elements that make the N24 so legendary – cars, drivers, girls, fans, fires, and the Nordschleife – and presents some fantastic footage from both sides of the fence. It’ll bring back great memories for any of you who attended, and make those who didn’t even more resolved to go next year. The on-board footage alone is epic; as a package it’s the perfect summary of another weekend well spent in the Eifel Mountains.
