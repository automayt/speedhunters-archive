In The Moment: Making An Entrance

June 14, 2013 Dino Dalle Carbonare

If I had to pick one image to sum up the insanity that is unfolding at Gatebil Mantorp right now, it would have to be a shot of one of the many Audi Quattros of various generations, ploughing into corners under lurid four-wheel drifts. It perfectly represents the mix of excessive power and pedal-to-the-metal driving style that defines these series of Scandinavian events. Best thing is, it’s only just started…
