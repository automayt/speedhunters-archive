If Mad Mike Lived In A LEGO World

December 23, 2013 Mike Garrett

I’m guessing that if you’re reading Speedhunters, there’s a good chance you’ve received LEGO as a Christmas gift at some point in your life. With Christmas just a couple of days away, it’s hard to think of something more appropriate to post than this incredible recreation of Mad Mike at a drift event, constructed completely out of the beloved plastic blocks.

I’m not sure what to say, other than that if this doesn’t put a grin on your face, I’m not sure what will.
