Wanted: Your American Iron

March 14, 2013 Mike Garrett

As we celebrate American car culture this month, we figured it would be a good time to revive the #FEATURETHIS program. Specifically, we are looking to showcase American-made cars owned by Speedhunters readers from around the world. If you have an American machine and would like to participate, send a few images (sized 800px or larger) to featurethis@dev.speedhunters.com . Please make sure to include some basic information about the car, as well as your name and location. We will gather up our favorites and post them up later in the month.

Looking forward to seeing what comes in!

-Mike
