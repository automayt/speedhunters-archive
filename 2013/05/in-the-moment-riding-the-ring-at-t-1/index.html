In The Moment: Riding The Ring At T-1

May 19, 2013 Jonathan Moore

There’s less than one hour to go before the 2013 Nürburgring 24 Hours, and Speedhunters have just had the ride of a lifetime. We’ve been riding the Nordschleife in a Bilstein-liveried Vantage following no less than James Bond himself, Daniel Craig, as part of a 100 car parade around the Nordschleife to celebrate the centenary of Aston Martin. Up front was Craig, Stirling Moss driving a vintage DBR1 and also the new Aston Martin concept car – more on that to come. What an introduction to the race!
