Discussion: Project Car Priorities

May 4, 2013 Mike Garrett

As my fellow Speedhunters and myself begin the process of digging into our project cars, I’ve had a little question in my head. Where do you personally like to start off when it comes to modifying a car? I know it will obviously depend on the type and condition of the car you are working with, but as a general rule what’s the bit you tackle first? Do you extract some extra power with bolt-on engine modifications? Liven up the sound with a better exhaust system? Do you go for a set of wheels? Do you upgrade the suspension for improved handling and a meaner stance?

I’ve personally gone with the latter choice on most of my cars, but I’m really curious to hear what you guys prefer. What’s your priority when it comes to modifying a car?

Let’s discuss!

-Mike
