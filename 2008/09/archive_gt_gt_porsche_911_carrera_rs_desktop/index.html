Archive&gt;&gt; Porsche 911 Carrera Rs Desktop

September 19, 2008 Antonio Alvendia

Just when you thought we were done posting stuff this week, I thought I'd just hit you up with this teaser photo…

Desktop, wallpaper, whatever you want to call it… to celebrate the
ongoing Porsche month on Speedhunters, I just uploaded a new addition
into our Speedhunters Desktops section. It's a photo of a pristine white 1973 Porsche 911 RS (a real RS from the Porsche museum, not a clone) that I shot in Germany, while we were on our Speedhunters Euro Trip.

This authentic RS was being driven hard up and down the Leipzig test track by
my new friend Manu, a Porsche test driver. He was smiling all day for
being given the opportunity to drive a real RS on the race track! Real
RS versions from 1973 are so rare, this type of thing doesn't happen
often… unless you're down with the Speedhunters crew.

Check out our desktops section to download this image, and check back on Speedhunters real soon for the full feature on this car!

-Antonio Alvendia
