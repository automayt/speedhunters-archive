Random Snap&gt;&gt;atenza Wagon

September 3, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

I guess I have kind of been on a wagon kick lately. Check out this Mazda Atenza (Mazda6) wagon I found on Minkara today. I have seen a lot of cool Atenzas in Japan and it makes me kind of want to buy one at some point. The owner of this particular wagon swapped out the stock front bumper for the OEM front bumper from the MazdaSpeed Atenza and it looks awesome. It rides low thanks to a set of Trust DMR coilovers and the the wheels are Advan Kreuzer Xi's in 19"x8.5J +45 in the front and +38 in the rear. This is as about as good as it gets and I think something like this would make an awesome daily driver.
