Video Roll&gt;&gt; Kyusha Parade

December 11, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

I think that perhaps all the Japanese car culture blogs kicking around the internet are creating a slightly distorted image of Japanese car culture for the casual viewer… if you go to Japan it is very unlikely you will see a one of these crazy Kyusha style cars….. they are quite the site though no? Pretty insane in all the right ways.

I’m going to be giving Tokyo Auto Salon a miss next month unfortunately… Too bad, as the sight of these cars is REALLY making me want to head over to Japan next January. It’s almost worth the trip just to hang out in the parking lot, to see these wild machines in the flesh!

Source: Japanese Nostalgic Car Blog
