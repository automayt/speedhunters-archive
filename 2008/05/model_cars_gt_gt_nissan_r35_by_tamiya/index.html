Model Cars&gt;&gt; Nissan R35 By Tamiya

May 21, 2008

 

During the biggest model car show in Japan, the Shizuoka Hobby Show, Tamiya debuted their 1/24 scale model kit Nissan R35. The kit consists of a engine bay and full interior details. You will need to assemble the complete kit, I tried building Tamiya cars before but I just don't have the patience and I always end up with something that doesn't even comes close to what you see in the instructions. I will be waiting for a fully assembled car.

Tamiya
