News&gt;&gt; Vw Scirocco Gt24

May 22, 2008 Andy Blackmore

More pictures have surfaced of the VW Scirocco racecar which will compete in this weekend's Nurburgring 24 Hour.

 

The Scirocco will be driven by two-time WRC champ Carlos Sainz and two-time Le Mans winner and 'Ring legend, Hans-Joachim Stuck. The racing bodykit really makes a difference to this car.


The presentation car carries a Nurburgring track graphic on the side of the car.
