Historic Masters Festival Brands Hatch Part 2 - On-track Photos

May 28, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

So we went to Brands Hatch at the weekend to check out the vintage machinery ripping round the famous GP circuit. After a good nose round the paddock we got track-side.

I always think it defeats the object to wear ear-plugs when you go to events like this but damn I should have worn some! These things make your inner ears melt…

Check some photos, I really should edit my selection down a bit more but never mind…

Lolas…


Don’t know much about these but I think they are Chevrons… nice looking though…



I love the burnt orange on this lola…

GT40’s are a fave

Onto the F1 stuff, love the Martini livery…

This V12 ex-Icxx Ferrari was soooo loud

Didn’t know Suzuki were ever in F1?!?

The Jag V10 was looking smart in this gold-on-black livery, first time a  modern era F1 car has hit Brands since the Eighties…

Started to push it after a couple of laps, it sounded so clean compared to the old-school engines…

Parade laps from some road-cars…  Yeah arches!!!

Not sure what the deal is with this aero kit? Bosozuku Corvette anyone??

Another Chevron, pretty neat…

Just noticed the number-plate on this Lola, must be road-legal WTF!

More than a little twitchy down paddock-hill…

Durex vs. Trojan…

These things were quick, honest…

So there you have it, hope you have enjoyed the photeees, til next time I’m out yo…

Tom
