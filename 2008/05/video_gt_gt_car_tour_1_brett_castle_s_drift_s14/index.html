Video&gt;&gt; Car Tour #1: Brett Castle&#039;s Drift S14

May 20, 2008

This past April, we travelled out to Round One of the European Drift Championship to check out the UK drifting scene.  The event was held at the famous race circuit Donington Park and was won by the 2007 European Drift Champion, Brett Castle. We were quite impressed with his drifting skills and also the build quality of his machine. What we were NOT impressed with was the weather! Ack!  It’s the first time I’ve seen snow and hail at a drifting event bleck. Froze my butt off! So anyway, this is the first Car Tour film we are releasing here on Speedhunters. You can see for yourself what one of the top UK drift spec S14s looks like.

Watch out for more Car Tours coming soon. We’ll be featuring different types of cars from across the planet over the course of the year: will be a range of drift, drag, circuit, production, Time Attack and modified street cars.  I’m pretty excited by the machines we’ll be getting to point our cameras at this year.

:Rod Chong
