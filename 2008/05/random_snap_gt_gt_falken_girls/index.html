Random Snap&gt;&gt; Golfcart X Falken Girls

May 27, 2008 Antonio Alvendia

One interesting kewinkydink… at Formula D events, I always seem to
be in the general vicinity of the Falken girls. I don't know why, it
just happens like that! Am I following them around the track? I dunno!
I prefer to think they're following my camera around! Ever since I had
that "I can make you famous" talk with them! Haha just kidding.

After sharing a Falken colored snow cone (green apple/berry punch
flavored, if you must know) up on the grid area, we decided to head
back to the pit area together. Since I'm always trying to fill my life
with excitement, I challenged Joyce (in the driver's seat) to a steep
downhill race back to the Falken booth, while Eri, Randyl, and CJ held
on for dear life! In fact, Randyl popped open her umbrella in an
attempt to slow Joyce down, but that didn't help at all. That's drag racing Randyl, we're at Formula D! Now flick that steering wheel and initiate, Joyce!!!

The above photo is proof of my multi-tasking abilities! (shooting
a long shutter speed photo, while racing Joyce downhill, and sipping on my snow
cone)

-Antonio Alvendia
