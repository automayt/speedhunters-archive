News&gt;&gt; Win A Ride In A Time-attack Impreza

August 22, 2008 Andy Blackmore

Thought this might interest Time-Attack fans in the UK, plus its for a good cause and celebrates the life of Colin McRae.

Roger Clark Motorsport is offering up a passenger ride in their 800bhp Subaru Impreza 'Gobstopper' in the Colin McRae Gathering convoy. The Gobstopper will be second in the Subaru only convoy behind McRae's championship winning Prodrive Subaru! As I write this, the highest bid is just 300GBP!

The McRae Gathering takes place on 31st August and  has been organised, with the full blessing of the McRae family and Prodrive as a tribute to Colin, who died last year. The Convoy starts at Lanark, Scotland (where Colin lived) to Banbury, Warwickshire, home of Prodrive. Almost 1300 Subaru's will take part in the convoy, which will set a new world record.

Please note, entry for the convoy is now closed, but it should be a great viewing and photographic opportunity – which you can share on the forums.

-Andy Blackmore
