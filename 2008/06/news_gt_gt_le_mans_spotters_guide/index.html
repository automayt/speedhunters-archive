News&gt;&gt; Le Mans Spotters Guide

June 11, 2008 Andy Blackmore

As you may have read on Speedhunters, I produce 'Spotters Guides' for various fanbsites, covering F1, BTCC, American Le Mans. These have proved very popular and with the support of Speedhunters, Radio Le Mans and DailySportscar.com I've been able to produce a Le Mans Spotters Guide!

As with the other Spotter Guides, this is available in a PDF and JPEG versions. The guide features side views of all the entries, along with team, chassis, engine, tyre and driver details to aid your viewing at La Sarthe, or in front of your television!

The PDF contains both pages in High Resolution format, so you can print this as big as you want, without loosing any resolution (dare someone to put it on the side of a truck!). The PDF is recommended for high-end printers and papers, larger than A4. You need Adobe Acrobat to view PDF files.

The two JPEG files are more suitable for A4/Letter and for older printers. Thanks to all the partners in helping to make the Spotters Guide series such as success.  

You can download the guides here –  http://www.spotterguides.com/
