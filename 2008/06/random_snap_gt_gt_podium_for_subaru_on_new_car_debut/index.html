News&gt;&gt; Podium For Subaru On New Car Debut

June 4, 2008 Andy Blackmore

A Follow up to our piece last week. The hard work by Prodrive and STI seems to be paying off as the Subaru WRC rally team scored a 2nd place with Petter Solberg at the Acropolis Rally last weekend. Team mate, Chris Atkinson retired, but did manage a stage win, another first for the new car.

-Andy Blackmore
