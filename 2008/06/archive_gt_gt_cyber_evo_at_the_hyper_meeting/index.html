Archive&gt;&gt;cyber Evo At The Hyper Meeting

June 17, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

I don't think the Cyber Evo time attack car needs much introduction here. Here we see Tarzan Yamada piloting the car towards the Dunlop bridge during the 2008 Hyper Meeting at Tsukuba Circuit. The car has been under constant improvement and I'm really excited to see what will happen once "time attack season" rolls around. The warm and humid summer months like this are not popular for time attack, but once things cool down in the fall the time attack action will start again.

Hyper Meeting

-Mike Garrett
