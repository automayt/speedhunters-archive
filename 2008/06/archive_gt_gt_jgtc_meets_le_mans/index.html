Archive&gt;&gt;jgtc Meets Le Mans

June 11, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

A pair of mid '90s era Honda NSX race cars. The car on the right is from the 1995 24H of Le Mans and the car on the left is a JGTC (Now Super GT) machine from roughly the same time period. I took this photo a couple years ago at the Honda Collection Hall. I was hoping to see the Le Mans car again when I was at the Collection Hall a few weeks ago but it wasn't there, at the time. Maybe they were tuning it up for a revival?

-Mike Garrett
