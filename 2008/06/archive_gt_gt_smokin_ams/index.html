Archive&gt;&gt;smokin&#039; Ams

June 6, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

The monster Evo 8 from AMS locks up the brakes heading into the "off ramp" at Buttonwillow during the 2007 Super Lap Battle final. With all the UK Time Attack stuff we have been running lately, I didn't want to leave out my fellow Americans. Last year a number of foreign teams brought their cars to Tsukuba Circuit to compete in the Rev Speed Time Attack, and I think it would be great to see some of the top US cars get a chance to run Tsukuba as well. I wonder how cars like the AMS Evo would do in comparison to the big Japanese tuners at Tsukuba?
