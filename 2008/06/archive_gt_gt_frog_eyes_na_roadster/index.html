Archive&gt;&gt;frog Eyes Na Roadster

June 24, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

As a former NA chassis Mazda Roadster owner, I will always have a soft spot for the original version of Mazda's iconic sports car. When it comes to NA Roadsters, the Frog Eyes circuit car has to be one of the coolest ever. From it's mega wide RS Watanabe wheels to its John Player Special livery homage, the car is just dripping with coolness. It also runs like a bat out of hell.

Here a few photos I snapped of the car at the R1 Grand Prix event last winter.
