Video&gt;&gt;slowmo Moment #13 The Colour Of Money

July 3, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

This is a bit of an epic slomo moment film coming in at 4 minutes duration. Perhaps a little self indulgent, but a must viewing for any purist drift car obsessive. The film shows the entire top 16 field from the recent Formula Drift New Jersey round as they roll in to the driver introductions…..

I want you to consider as you watch this just how many of these cars have the full backing of a factory car or tire team….. it's pretty full on….. Also an amazing variety of machines. The only thing we are missing is a drifting BMW.

This is a whole other level of professional high end drifting….. truly a sight to behold!

An amazing collection of cars, drivers and builds…. Notice that some of the drivers have "passengers"…. BTW If you download the HD version you can really see the expressions on the driver's faces and get a sense of their personalities.

I love it!

:Rod Chong
