Here is a quick snap shot (quite literally) of the official elimination table for tomorrow's Red Bull Drifting World Championship. Should be amazing day!

1: Rhys Millen: no surprizes there…

2: Samuel Hubinette: the FD drift royalty comes out on top….

3: Paul Vlasblom: BIG NEWS! he had the crowd screaming and yelling with his wall proximity and 100% foot to the floor style.

4: Katsuhiro Ueo: his drift initiations were amazing.

5: Darren McNamara: he looked fast and aggressive.
