Random Snap&gt;&gt; Bmw 2002

October 29, 2008 TheSpeedhunters

I moved to a new apartment in a different city not too long ago, and it seems that there are some serious BMW affiniados here. I have seen a lot of BMW 02's lately, I have really started to like these cars. I remember Antonio  found a BMW 2002 when we were in Spa to shoot the 24hrs race, he showed me some pictures on his camera and since then I have been looking for some nice examples in the neighborhood. The car in the picture is based in Australia and rocks some solid go fast parts like the rollcage and throttle bodies under the hood. Also notice the Japanese 5zigen FN01R rims.

-Jeroen Willemsen
