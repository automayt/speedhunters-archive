Are You Ready To Experience The Green Hell?

March 30, 2016 Misha Charoudin

The car season is slowly kicking off again in Europe, and Ring Garage has something to really get it started with a bang. As Speedhunters’ official Nürburgring partner, this can only mean one thing: the chance for an EU-based reader to win the Ultimate Nürburgring Experience.

Always wanted to lap the ‘Ring? What about experiencing the thrill of a race from inside a race car? Or perhaps you think traveling sideways is the fastest line? Why choose one when you can have it all and much more?!

The prize is pretty substantial: 3 full days of petrolhead activities at the Green Hell for you and a friend from the 28th to 30th of April 2016!

Speaking of friends, on the 29th of April the Carmrades in the Ring CT event will be rocking the Nürburgring and its surroundings. Hundreds of petrolheads will be sharing the passion for cars, and Speedhunters will be there to cover the event and the lucky winner’s experience.

Enough talking though – click here to check out the competition details. See you at the ‘Ring!
