Time For A Video Break...

March 13, 2016 TheSpeedhunters

A new week of Speedhunting is about to kick off, but before we get into it, it’s time to take a look at some of the videos we’ve been checking out over the past 7 days.

Let’s get things started with a new clip from Geibunsha Japan…

Coinciding with a new issue of Stance magazine, this video focuses on a pair of Audis that have been collaboratively restyled by Liberty Walk in the company’s signature style. That is, wide and very low. Included is a short interview with Wataru Kato, in which he shares his thoughts on just how shakotan cars should be driven.

Next up, it’s a nicely produced clip from Top Gear magazine, showcasing TG‘s head test driver Ollie Marriage’s thrash of the epic, track-only 820hp Aston Martin Vulcan around the Abu Dhabi GP circuit. Do we need to say any more?

Whether drag racing is your thing or not, this new clip by Dirt Shark is worth a look. The cameras roll as Brittany Force tests her Monster Energy Top Fuel Dragster at Wild Horse Pass Motorsports Park in Phoenix, Arizona, ahead of the 2016 NHRA season, along the way providing some great insight into how the team works. Brittany’s father, drag racing icon and king of sponsor endorsements, John Force, makes a few appearances too, doing what he does best of course.

Finally this week we have short time-lapse video from Prodrive, centered on building an Aston Martin V8 Vantage GTE for the 2016 FIA World Endurance Championship. Although it appears to have been captured either by a security camera or a potato, it’s neat to see the three-week build come together in just 60 seconds.

As always, if you’ve watched a cool clip online this week, don’t forget to share it in the comments section below!
