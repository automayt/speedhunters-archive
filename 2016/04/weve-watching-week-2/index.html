What We’ve Been Watching This Week...

April 24, 2016 TheSpeedhunters

A new week of Speedhunting is about to kick off, but before that happens it’s time to take a quick look at some of the videos we’ve been watching over the past seven days.

First up, we have a touching clip that was born out of a YouTube comment to a video discussing whether video games can be a spiritual experience…

 Player Two is the reenactment of a story told by YouTube user 00WARTHERAPY00, involving the early ’00s game RalliSport Challenge and a saved ‘ghost lap’ set by his late father.

Next up it’s another great clip from Petrolicious, this time with a classic Nissan Skyline DR30 as the subject matter. The angular ’80s coupe has become a labor of love for its owner Jay Kho, and with an SR20DET now under the hood and a host of other modifications, it’s his dream car epitomized.

If you follow pro-level drifting in Europe and the US, you’ll most likely be familiar with the name Kristaps Blušs and his company HGK Motorsport – both of which are no strangers to Speedhunters. On the off chance that you’re not, you definitely will be after watching this new clip from Robert Vitols, aptly titled Badass Latvian.

Finally this week we have a very nicely produced clip from I Love Bass. If you enjoyed their Dubshed official video we showcased last week, this teaser for the forthcoming FittedUK event should strike a chord too. We can’t wait for the post-event release.

That’s all for now, but as always, if you’ve watched a cool automotive-related video this week, please share it with us in the comments section below!
