What We’ve Been Watching...

May 29, 2016 TheSpeedhunters

A new week (and month) of Speedhunting is about to kick off, but before that happens, it’s time to take a quick look at some of the videos we’ve been watching over the past seven days.

First up, we have a new short film from EKanoo Racing…

We’ve seen plenty of fast ETs being set by the Bahrain-based drag racing team over the past few years, but some they’re most proud of are those run by their R35 Nissan GT-R. And rightly so – there is no quicker GT-R or all-wheel drive machine on the planet. Shot last month, this 8-minute video takes a closer look at EKanoo’s GT-R campaign.

Next up it’s a very cool clip from Porsche, featuring 20 cars and a single lap of Mazda Raceway Laguna Seca – POV style. Compiled from HD footage obtained at last September’s Rennsport Reunion event, you’ll definitely want to crank up the volume on this one.

In the custom car world, Rad Rides by Troy needs little introduction; the Illinois-based workshop is the name behind some amazing machines. In this new video from Motorhead magazine (Japan), we get a closer look at the facility and some of the cars that Troy Trepanier and his team have built.

Last, but certainly not least this week, is the first episode in a new series from Blackbird Automotive. The subject of this stunning short film is one of the most beautiful endurance race cars of all time: the Ferrari 512S.

That’s all for now, but as always, if you’ve watched a cool automotive-related video this week, please share it with us in the comments section below.
