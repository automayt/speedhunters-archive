Green Hell? All We Can See Is Asphalt Heaven

July 29, 2016 Mark Riccioni

12.93 miles, 154 turns and a record average speed of 125.17mph. Is there any greater test of man and machine than a lap of the Nordschleife?

Last week we joined ex-BTCC mechanic Ryan Stewart as he prepared to tackle the Green Hell for the first time. His weapon of choice? A GT3-inspired Porsche 987 Cayman clad with semi-slick tyres and Bilstein Clubsport suspension.

Ryan’s initial thoughts: “Forget what you think you know about the Nordschleife – no amount of YouTube videos of virtual laps can prepare you for your first outing. This place demands respect.”
