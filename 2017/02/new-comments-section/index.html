Fighting The Bots: The Comments Section Reborn

February 8, 2017 Dino Dalle Carbonare

As you may have noticed over the last week or so, our comments section has been under severe and constant attack by spambots. And the more we delete, the more we get spammed on every post we publish.

We’ve had our best guys on the case and have now implemented some changes to the spam filter that will put an end to it all. This will allow us to no longer depend on third parties for our comments section, and with the new system you can now sign up using your social logins to make things easier.

I look forward to chatting to you all in the new and improved comments section!
