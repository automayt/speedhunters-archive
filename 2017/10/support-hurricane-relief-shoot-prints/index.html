Support Hurricane Relief With A Shoot, Prints &amp; More

October 10, 2017 TheSpeedhunters

As the days count down to the championship series’ finale at Irwindale Speedway, a Formula Drift charity initiative is set to support victims of recent hurricanes. And Speedhunters’ own Larry Chen is donating to the cause by auctioning off a photoshoot and a series of signed prints.

The one-hour private shoot for one vehicle with Master Chen, to take place in the greater Los Angeles area during December is one of many unique items currently being auctioned off by Formula Drift on eBay. All proceeds raised will benefit those affected by the disasters via non-profit charity organizations.

This is a great initiative to get behind, but get in quick as all the auctions will come to an end on October 15 at 12:00am.
