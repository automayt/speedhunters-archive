Video Roll&gt;&gt; The Millens Test Their Hyundais For Pikes Peak

June 13, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRDbW98wLiA:600:375]

Earlier today, we saw the Dacia Duster prototype be put through its paces as it preps for Pikes Peak. Now, we get to see another unlimited class contender, Rhys Millen's Hyundai Genesis RMR PM580, do the same. Last year, Rhys was struggling with the car all week long on the mountain, but it seems like all the bugs have ironed out and looks to be a quite a competitor this year.

After 11 years, Rod Millen will also be making a comeback to the mountain and will be driving Rhys' Formula Drift car. Pikes Peak is definitely shaping up to be quite an event.

-Linhbergh
