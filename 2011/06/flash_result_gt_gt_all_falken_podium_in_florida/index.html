Flash Result&gt;&gt; An All Falken Podium In Florida

June 4, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

As the heat and humidity subsided and the action at Palm Beach International Raceway came to an end, a winner had to be picked and Justin Pawlak is the one. Vaughn Gittin Jr. took second and Darren McNamara stood on third. An all Falken podium and the second all ASD podium. Congratulations to Justin and to all of Team Falken!

-Linhbergh

P.S. That's JTP's wife giving him the passionate victory kiss.
