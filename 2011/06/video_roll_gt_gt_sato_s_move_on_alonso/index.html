Video Roll&gt;&gt; Sato&#039;s Move On Alonso

June 12, 2011 Charles Kha

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRJwZu5Qqio:600:375]

The Canadian GP isn't renowned for overtaking. Besides the hairpin, opportunities to dive up the inside of someone are few and far between at Circuit Gilles Villeneuve. Takuma Sato however, didn't get that particular memo. In 2007, Sato – in a Super Aguri – grabbed a tow off the back of Fernando Alonso's McLaren and made an audacious move going into the final chicane. Fernando Alonso isn't an easy guy to pass, so for Sato to attempt a pass on the outside left a lasting impression.

It'll be very interesting to see how much spice KERS and DRS adds to today's race!

- Charles Kha
