News&gt;&gt;porsche Return To Le Mans In 2014

June 30, 2011 Andy Blackmore

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhOsDzSJ0bk:600:375]

The most successful manufacturer in Le Mans, Porsche have announced they will be returning to Le Mans with a Le Mans Prototype racecar in time for the 2014 running of the legendary event. Regulations for the 2014 season still need to be confirmed, but it would not be a surprise if Hybrid technology was employed given the Porsche 911 GT3 Hybrid and the recent Porsche 918 RSR Concept.

Reports suggest the decision is independent of Audi AG who have yet to confirm their future.

Porsche's Le Mans victories were recently celebrated by an Art Car livery on the Flying Lizard Porsche 911 GT3 RSR at Le Mans… obviously a prelude to this announcement.

-Andy Blackmore
