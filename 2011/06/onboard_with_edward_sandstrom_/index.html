June 3, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AUNoHRuffiY:600:375]

After a brief hiatus, the GT3 European Championship returned to Silverstone this weekend. In a close fought Race 1 today, The Team Need for Speed BMW Z4 driven by Edward Sandstrom/Abdulaziz al-Faisal finished 4th and Csaba Walter/Claudia Hurtgen finished 6th. 

As a treat we've uploaded some raw, unedited footage of Edward Sandström's practice lap on the new Silverstone circuit. So turn up your speakers, sit back and watch Edward push the
Team Need For Speed BMW Z4 GT3 to the limit!
