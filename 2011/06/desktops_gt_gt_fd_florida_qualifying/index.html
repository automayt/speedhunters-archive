Desktops&gt;&gt; Fd Florida Qualifying

June 4, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

As the Charles, Larry, and I head to the Palm Beach International Raceway for the Formula D top 32 battles that will go underway shortly, I thought I'd put up a desktop selection from yesterday's qualifying action. 

Fredric Aasbo's wingless Scion tC was looking fantastic. He was billowing out and insane amount of tire smoke!

Running off the high from his great finish in Atlanta, John Russakoff showed us his best '86 swagger. 

Finally, there's this shot of Ryan Tuerck's Camaro sporting his new red anodized Enkei RPF-1s.

Head on over to the desktops section to grab your wallpaper selects. It's onwards to the Top 32 for us!

-Linhbergh
