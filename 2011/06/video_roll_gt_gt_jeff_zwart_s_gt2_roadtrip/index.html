Video Roll&gt;&gt; Jeff Zwart&#039;s Gt2 Rs Roadtrip

June 25, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

[View:http://vimeo.com/25492091:600:375]

One of the great stories to come out of this year's Pikes Peak Hill Climb is the story of Jeff Zwart and his red Porsche 911 GT2 RS. He drove the street-legal Porsche 1,132 miles from Porsche Motorsports North America in Santa Ana, California to Pikes Peak and will drive it on race day. Tomorrow, he will be defending and trying to break his 

time attack record from Rod Millen.

This film by Will Roegge tells the story of the road trip to the peak.

-Linhbergh
