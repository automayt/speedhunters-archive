Video Roll&gt;&gt; Bmw M3 Crt

June 24, 2011 Charles Kha

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Op96Pz4OrYw:600:375]

Standard M3 a bit too vanilla for you? Then perhaps the BMW M3 CRT is right up your alley. CRT stands for Carbon Racing Technology, and the limited edition M3 benefits from a CFRP hood and seats. A titanium muffler and lightweight sound deadening also help shed the pounds off the M3 CRT, while the V8 features increased torque and power output thanks to a larger displacement. Sadly, only only 67 will be made.

- Charles Kha
