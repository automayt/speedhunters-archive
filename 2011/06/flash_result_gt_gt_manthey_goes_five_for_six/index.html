Flash Result&gt;&gt; Manthey Goes Five For Six

June 26, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

After witnessing the dominance of the Manthey Racing cars in previous years, I wasn't surprised to watch them once again battle their way to the top. Today's win at the 2011 Nürburgring 24HR makes it their fifth win in six years, with last year's win by BMW the only dent in this incredibly impressive record. We'll be bringing you more coverage from the event shortly.

-

Paddy McGrath

Provisional Result after 24 hours :

1. #18 Manthey Racing Porsche 997 GT3R – Marc Lieb, Lucas Luhr, Timo Bernhard & Romain Dumas
2. #1 BMW Motorsport M3 GT – Jörg Müller, Augusto Farfus, Uwe Alzen & Pedro Lamy
3. #14 Audi Sport Team Phoenix R8 LMS – Marc Basseng, Marcel Fässler, Andrea Piccini & Frank Stippler
