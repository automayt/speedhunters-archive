In The Moment&gt;&gt; Only 23 Hours 59 To Go

June 11, 2011 Jonathan Moore

This was the moment when 30,000bhp came straight at me at 150mph. For over a minute the cars streamed through: the LM Prototypes up front followed by the hordes of GT cars, spread out three and four wide on the run to the first corner and the rise up to the Dunlop chicane. One minute down: only 23 hours 59 to go.

Jonathan Moore
