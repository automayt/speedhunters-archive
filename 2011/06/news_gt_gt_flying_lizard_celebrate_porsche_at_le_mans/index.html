News&gt;&gt;flying Lizard Celebrate Porsche At Le Mans

June 1, 2011 Andy Blackmore

Flying Lizard Motorsport will be celebrating Porsche Le Mans wins at this year's race with this stunning livery, created by Troy Lee Designs.

The livery celebrates featuring the 28 drivers who have won the legendary event (overall), driving a Porsche.

The drivers’ national flags and names flow over the car as if in a wind tunnel and are integrated into the traditional Flying Lizard graphic on the side. An amazing design.

Legends, who include Hans Hermann and Richard Attwood winners of the 1970 Edition of Le Mans are alongside other drivers including Derek Bell, Jacky Ickx, Henri Pescarolo, Klaus Ludwig, Hans Stuck.

Current Le Mans drivers, Allan McNish, Tom Kristensen and Alex Wurz also feature. Quite the line up!

The team have two entries in the event. The #80 GTE-Pro car is finished in Candy Metallic Orange with a Deep Ocean Blue rear end and has the strong driver line up of Patrick Long, Jörg Bergmeister and Lucas Luhr. 

The second car is in a vibrant Candy Metallic Blue with the Ocean Blue rear and is entered in the GTE-Am class where Pro drivers are partnered with semi-pro (they aren’t really amateurs!) drivers. Team owner Seth Neiman, Darren Law and Spencer Pumpelly pilot the Blue machine.

Flying Lizard and Troy Lee Designs ran a special one off Lizard livery at Le Mans in 2007 and regarded as one of the best liveries seen on a 911 in recent years.

Green Flag for the 2011 24 Hour of Le Mans drops at 3pm local time on June 11. The race will be broadcast on Eurosport in Europe and Speed TV in North America. Radio Le Mans will be providing full in-depth coverage of Le Mans during the week and we will have Jonathan Moore at the event.

-Andy Blackmore
