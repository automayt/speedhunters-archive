Flash Result&gt;&gt; Grunewald Stands On Top At Fd Jersey

June 18, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Wall Stadium became a grave yard of drift cars. Battle after battle, drift cars left and right kept slamming into the railing on the bank and after a while became a game of survival. with bits of carnage littered the track. And Conrad Grunewalds and his Hankook Tire Chevy Camaro survived and took the win for the event. Justin Pawlak took second and Daijiro Yoshihara took third. Congrats to all the winners.

-Linhbergh
