Random Snap&gt;&gt; Honda Beat On Te37

November 10, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

This little Honda Beat on black TE37s was blasting around Sodegaura Forest on Sunday and despite not being particularly fast compared to other cars there, it looked like a real blast! Pocket-sized Kei sports cars like this were all the rage back in the nineties, offering up fun and affordable performance, not to mention plenty of tunability. It might be hard these days for manufacturers to justify cars like these, but wouldn't it be cool to see what a modern day Honda Beat or Suzuki Cappuccino would look..and drive like?

Sodegaura Forest

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
