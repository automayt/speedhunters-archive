Comic&gt;&gt; The Norwegian Hammer

November 4, 2011 Charles Kha

Before we resume our regular programming, we have the final installment of our Team Need for Speed comic series. This time, the focus is on Fredric Aasbo at Formula DRIFT Irwindale. 

Yesterday we released Matt Powers' comic – illustrated by Josh Awesome – which was done in a French cartoon style. For Fredric Aasbo's comic we called upon fellow Need for Speed The Run artist, John Yip.

John consumes a heavy dose of Japanese manga on a daily basis, and this influence has filtered through to his drawing style.

I hope you enjoy reading these comics as much as we enjoyed making them!

- Charles Kha
