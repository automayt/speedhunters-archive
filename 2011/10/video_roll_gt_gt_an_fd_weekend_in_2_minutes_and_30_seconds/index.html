Video&gt;&gt; An Fd Weekend In 2 Minutes And 30 Seconds

October 12, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

A whole lot goes on during a Formula Drift weekend. There’s the initial load in of all the different teams’ rigs on Thursday, the countless workers that help set up the track, the drifting, and of course, the throngs among throngs of spectators. It’s hard to imagine all that happens, but Larry Chen does quite the number and compresses a whole weekend at Irwindale Speedway, all its happenings, in two minutes and thirty seconds.

-Linhbergh
