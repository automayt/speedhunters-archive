Desktops&gt;&gt; Formula 1 At Night

October 3, 2011 Charles Kha

Formula 1 at night is truly stunning. You see things that you wouldn't otherwise: sparks, the drivers' eyes and glowing brake discs. If you weren't able to attend this year's Singapore Grand Prix, held at night on the streets of Marina Bay, then head on over to our Desktops Section.

Post-race: the marbles on a used Pirelli slick.

Mark Webber negotiates traffic through the tunnel, with Fernando Alonso hot on his heels.

The Australian would finish the race third.

Seven time World Champion Michael Schumacher fends off Lewis Hamilton.

Spent rubber from Jarno Trulli's Team Lotus T128.

Sebastian Vettel out front and all alone.

After colliding with Massa and receiving a drive-through penalty, Lewis Hamilton put in a series of quick laps to recover to fifth. Here he uses all of the track and then some.

Vettel leads the field on the opening lap, with Nico Rosberg taking a shortcut in the background.

Jenson Button passes Webber off the line and begins his pursuit of the other Red Bull.

JB was also caught dancing his McLaren against the wall in Practice.
