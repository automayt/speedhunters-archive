Car Spotlight&gt;&gt; The Farm Truck Sleeper

October 27, 2011 Charles Kha

One of the better known sleepers in the US is the 'Farm Truck'. Based in Oklahoma, this Chevrolet C10 pickup seems to have no place on the drag strip. And it certainly isn't a vehicle you'd expect to see laying down a 10-sec pass.

The truck hit notoriety after its appearance in Pinks All Out where it ran down the quarter mile in 10.53 @ 125.56mph.

Powering the pickup a 502 cubic inch big block. A healthy dose of giggle gas helps it down the quarter. 

ZZZZZZ: undoubtedly the perfect plate for this sleeper.

Giving the game away are the Hoosier drag slicks on the rear.

Hidden within the rear canopy is the roll cage. I'm assuming the spare tyres are for ballast?

The man responsible for this pickup is Sean from Pimp Juice, a brand of traction fluid.

Further humiliating its quarter mile opponents is the Papa Angelo's Pizzeria sign that's stuck on its roof. Truly an amazing sleeper.

- Charles Kha
