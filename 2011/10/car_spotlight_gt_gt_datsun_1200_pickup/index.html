Car Spotlight&gt;&gt; Clean Datsun 1200 Pickup

October 6, 2011 Casey Dhnaram

Moving onto our second spotlight from Jamboree, we have this little Datsun 1200 pickup truck to show you. It was parked among a whole line of clean 1200s, of which any were good enough to be featured here on Speedhunters. In the end it was the vibrant red colour which drew me in.

Under the hood, the standard motor is still in place and was spotless in its presentation.

The interior was also concourse clean.

I have to be honest, I've always had a soft spot for little pickup trucks such as this 1200 and seeing this one has made me want one even more.

The car is sitting on a set of 14" Riversides wrapped in Nankang 175/60 rubber giving it a very classic Japanese look.

This Japanese sticker printed on the front wind-shield translates to 'Sunny Trucks'.

The rear tray has a substantial amount of room for those every day jobs too, what a perfect daily this would be.

Here is one last shot of it sitting next to more Datsun pickups.

-Casey
