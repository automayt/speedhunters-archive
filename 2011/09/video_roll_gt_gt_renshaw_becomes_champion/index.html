Video Roll&gt;&gt; Renshaw Becomes Champion

September 28, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Last weekend saw the final of the 2011 Time Attack Series in the UK. Gavin Renshaw, piloting the NR Autosport Mitsubishi Lancer Evo brought the car around the Snetterton circuit in a-not-very-slow 1:16.6 in the final session of the season. It was enough for him to take the win on the day and secure the 2011 Pro Class Time Attack Series championship. We'll have more on the event in the coming days …

-

Paddy McGrath
