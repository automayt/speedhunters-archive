News&gt;&gt; Course Change For Fd Irwindale

September 30, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

It's hard to believe that the 2011 Formula D season is coming to a close. Next weekend will be the 8th time Formula D has finished a season at Irwindale Speedway,  the "House of Drift." To freshen things up and make the show more exciting this year, the organizers decided to change the competition course. This new course is longer, faster and will be much more entertaining to the fans while bringing new challenges to the drivers and teams.

Will this new course change up with pecking order? Or will the top tier teams dominate even more? We'll be going into Irwindale knowing that a new champion will be crowned and this change will definitely spice things up.

-Linhbergh
