Video Roll&gt;&gt; Kumakubo Drifting The Laurel

April 18, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u5-BqDMEW08&feature=youtu.be:600:375]

After last week's shakedown tests in Ebisu East course Kumakubo was out on Minami with his new Laurel yesterday. The guys from Powervehicles captured his first drift attempts, as he slowly unleashed the power the highly tuned RB26 develops. It's still unknown if the car will make it to Autopolis for the first round this weekend but the guys at Team Orange are really trying their best to get things set-up and working. They were having an issue with the interior of the car filling up with thick tire smoke, something that is being take care of now. Hit play and hear that RB scream!
