Video Roll&gt;&gt; Fatlace Tv: Rwb Nakai-san

August 18, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

With about a month and a half to go before the Hellaflush Japan meeting in Fuji Speedway the guys at Fatlace have teamed up with Nakai-san and the RWB team to make sure some of the coolest Porsches in Japan will be making it to the event. To celebrate this partnership filmaker Luke Huxham was called upon to spend a day with Nakai-san and do a short interview to show the world what RWB is all about. Hit play and immerse yourselves in yet more RWB magic.
