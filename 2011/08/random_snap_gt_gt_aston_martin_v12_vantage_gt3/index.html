Random Snap&gt;&gt;aston Martin V12 Vantage Gt3

August 24, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Aston Martin just revealed their new V12 Vantage GT3 car. As you would expect from Aston Martin, the new machine is an exercise in race car in beauty. The dry-sump V12 will make 600 horsepower and if you are looking to campaign one of these cars yourself, prices start at just under £300,000. Testing will begin in October and the finished products should be in the hands of racers by early 2012.

-Mike Garrett
