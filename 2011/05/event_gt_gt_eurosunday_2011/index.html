Event&gt;&gt; Eurosunday 2011

May 17, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Time for some Euro love with this report from EuroSunday in Belgium. Cars from different countries drove to the event to see what's hot and what's not. The Golf MKII above is definitely hot in my book.

These Bentley wheels never looked better on a Audi TT. A perfect combination and flawlessly executed.

Rotiform looks to be unstoppable in their quest for world domination. This Belgium Scirocco wears the Rotiform BRU with great pride.

A great example of a great car. This 3 series with airbags and classic BBS wheels would look perfect in my dream garage.

Some people wouldn't even consider painting a car brown. But this MKII shows it can make your car a real head turner.

The MKII can be picked up cheap nowadays. All the money you save can be poured into wheels, suspension and the engine.

Volkswagen really outdone themselves with the Scirocco; the only drawback is the pricetag.

This BMW also got the Rotiform treatment with a set of what looks like three piece NUE wheels.

A classic in the making.

A car that already reached classic status is this Mercedes Benz 250S.

This Vauxhall (also known as Opel) Corsa came all the way from the UK to show off it's perfect stance.

‘Whoa' is all can say. I hope to see this car at M.I.V.W. later on in the year.

Old school VW bug without rusted panels and old paint looks real refreshing.

Zender Turbo wheel adore this beige MKII.

This Honda Civic managed to sneak past the entry guards but I'm sure a lot of people wouldn't have mind looking at it.

Thanks to Ronald Veth for the use of his pictures.

-Jeroen Willemsen
