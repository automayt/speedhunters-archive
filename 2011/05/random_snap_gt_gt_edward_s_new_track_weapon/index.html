Random Snap&gt;&gt; Edward&#039;s New Track Weapon

May 2, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

If one considers motorsport to be a somewhat polite form of warfare, then Edward Sandstrom and Team NFS Schubert Racing must be feeling a just wee bit confident right now. I’m sure you would to, if you had such a formidable new weapon in your possession! Yes ladies and gentlemen, I present  the completely revamped BMW Z4 GT3, now complete with Team Need for Speed livery. Wow…

Fellow Speedhunter, Jonathan Moore and I will be on site to witness the debut of the new car this weekend in Portugal. Plans are to stream all the GT1 and GT3 races on Speedhunters too, so where ever you live, there’s no reason to miss the on-track action.

Race times:

Saturday, May 7th:

FIA GT3 European Championship Race 1: 14:15 to 15:15 CET

FIA GT1 World Championship Qualifying Race: 17:15 to 18:15 CET

Sunday, May 8th:

FIA GT3 European Championship Race 2: 11:15 to 15:15 CET

FIA GT1 World Championship Race: 15:15 to 16:15 CET

I can’t wait!

:Rod Chong
