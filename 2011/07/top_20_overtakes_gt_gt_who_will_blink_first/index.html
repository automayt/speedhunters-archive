Top 20: Overtakes&gt;&gt; Who Will Blink First?

July 26, 2011 Charles Kha

Lewis Hamilton has been in the press a fair bit over the past few weeks, primarily because of his questionable overtaking moves in the Monaco GP and then his clash with team-mate Jenson Button in the Canadian GP. In spite of the heat coming from his fellow drivers, F1 pundits and the media in general, Hamilton has stayed true to his beliefs and said he won't change his aggressive driving style.

And those of you who have followed him since his GP2 days will know that he's telling the truth. Case in point: this never-say-die move from the 2006 Silverstone GP2 race where Lewis Hamilton challenged Piccione and Piquet Jr to a racing equivalent of a game of chicken, edging them out in one of the most challenging parts of the circuit.

- Charles Kha
