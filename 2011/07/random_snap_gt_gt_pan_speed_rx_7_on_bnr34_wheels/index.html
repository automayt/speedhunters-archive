Random Snap&gt;&gt; Pan Speed Rx-7 On Bnr34 Wheels

July 12, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

When I headed to Daikoku Futo to see the Japanese entries to this year's WTAC being unloaded onto the port, there was one car that really grabbed my attention and it wasn't one of the four time attack cars. It was this FD3S Totsuka-san of Pan Speed had rolled in with, a very nice looking A-spec running BNR34 GT-R stock 18-inch wheels. I often see these wheels being used by drifters on a variety of cars, but it's not often you see them fitted to RX-7s. I thought they fit the car rather well, definitely unique! 

Pan Speed

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
