Car Spotlight&gt;&gt;rsr For The Street

July 21, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

During my recent visit to Cars & Coffee, I once again came across a stunning 911 that needs to be shared. This 1978 930 has been converted into a street-going RSR/934 replica with all the style you'd expect of a vintage Porsche race car.

With only 32,000 total miles on the chassis and just 12,000 since its resto – the car is in immaculate condition. Thanks to its Leaf Green paintwork, it's has been affectionately dubbed "The Frog". The angriest, sexiest frog ever that is.

Thanks to some engine tuning and a healthy amount of boost, horsepower figures are said to be right around 420.

917 style centerlock wheels with Michelin rubber define the race car look.

As do the 934 rear hips.

It's not a trailer queen either. The car has made the journey to Porsche events all around California.

Like it? Love it? Want to own it? Check out AutoKennel, where it's being offered for sale at the about same price as a new base model 911 Carrera.

New or old? Take your pick.

-Mike Garrett
