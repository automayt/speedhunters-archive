Video Roll&gt;&gt; One Lap Of La Sarthe With The 787b

July 9, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

A while back we saw actor, Patrick "McDreamy" Dempsey, take it easy around the Le Mans race track in the new restored Mazda 787b. But on race day of this year's 24 Hours of Le Mans, Johnny Herbert returned, once again, to give the full beans to his Le Mans winning car for the first time since that historic day in 1991.

Turn up the volume, sit back, and enjoy the glorious sound of the 26B as it thunders down the Mulsanne Straight.

-Linhbergh
