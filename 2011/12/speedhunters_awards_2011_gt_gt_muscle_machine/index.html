Speedhunters Awards 2011&gt;&gt;muscle Machine Of The Year

December 16, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Over the course of our discussions on this year's Speedhunters Awards, we decided that a couple new categories should be added. There's nothing quite like a Muscle Car, and given the number of them featured this year, we felt these Muscle Machines deserve their own category.

Here's our inaugural list of nominees for Muscle Machine of the Year.

Camaro SS Turbo

Capri Perana V8

Ford Fairmont 5.0

The Innovator

Jon Sibal's Dodge Challenger

Mary Pozzi's Camaro

NASCAR Style Dodge Daytona

Need For Speed RTR-X

Need For Speed Shelby Super Snake

Passionatley Pink Camaro

Pontiac Grand Am Street Machine

Ring Brothers Diversion Camaro

The Roadrattler

Sam Posey Dodge Challenger Trans Am Racer

Spectre Camaro

Spectre Ford Maverick

Please make your vote below, and remember that votes in the comment section will NOT be counted.
