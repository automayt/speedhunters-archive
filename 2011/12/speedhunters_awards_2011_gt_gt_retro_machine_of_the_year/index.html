Speedhunters Awards 2011&gt;&gt; Retro Machine Of The Year

December 19, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

As you can probably see, our awards season is now well and truly underway. This is the first time where I've had the chance to become actively involved with the awards process. This time last year, I was too busy pulling my hair out creating the prototype art book. Having had the chance to look through the categories so far, it never ceases to amaze me the breadth of car culture we bring together here on Speedhunters. The nominations for Retro Car of the Year reflect this quite well I think. From street cars to race cars, concours to cat and from modified to stock – the Retro Car of the Year will surely divide opinions. For the first time, Hot Rods, Muscle Cars and Customs will have their own seperate category. Mike will be along with those polls shortly. For now, I'm going to risk damaging my brain cell whilst trying to decide what to vote for below …
