Speedhunters Awards 2011&gt;&gt; Pro Drift Car Of The Year

December 14, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

For the next category in the Speedhunters Awards, we have a selection of professional drift cars that made waves this year. Some of these cars were all new for 2011, others were completely rebuilt for the season. Take a look at the nominees we've selected and make your pick for 2011's Pro Drift Car of the Year.

Here are the nominees -

Chris Forsberg's Nissan 370Z

Cole Armstrong's 4-door R34 Skyline

Curt Whittaker's 2JZ R34 Skyline

Dai Yoshihara's S13 Silvia

Daigo Saito's JZX100 Chaser

Darren McNamara's DMac86

Darren McNamara's Saturn Sky

Fredric Aasbo's Euro Supra

Fredric Aasbo's Scion tC

James Deane's LBD PS13

Justin Pawlak's Ford Mustang

Kunny’Z D1 Mark X

Mad Mike's RX-7

Matt Powers' V8 S14

Mike Essa's BMW Z4

RE-Amemiya D1 RX-8

Ryan Tuerck's Chevy Camaro

SATS Cosworth Supra

Tanaka's 2JZ S15 Silvia

Team Orange Laurel

Tyler McQuarrie's 350Z

Make your choice in the poll below and remember that votes in the comments section will NOT be counted.

- Mike Garrett
