Random Snap&gt;&gt;tough Ae70

December 27, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

I was browsing through Facebook this morning when I found this awesome AE70 Sprinter that Manabu Mitsumori posted up. If you don't remember, he's the one who built that wicked JZX100 Chaser that we featured a while back. This is his latest project.

Mitsumori-san has a keen sense of style, and it shows here. Besides the fresh bodywork and Panasport wheels, it's also sporting a 20-valve 4AGE swap under the hood.

-Mike Garrett
