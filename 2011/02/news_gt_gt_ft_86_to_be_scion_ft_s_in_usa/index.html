News&gt;&gt;ft-86 To Be Scion Ft-s In Usa?

February 14, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

The net has been abuzz lately with news and rumors about everyone's favorite concept car, the Toyota FT-86. The latest news is that the car will be known as the Scion FT-S when it hits dealers in the United States, not the FR-S as previously speculated. The news also suggests that the FT-S will be powered by a 2.5 liter engine instead of 2.0.

What do you guys think about FT-S as a name for this hugely anticipated car?

Whatever the name is, we just want to drive it! We should know more after the US-spec car debuts at the New York Auto Show.

-Mike Garrett
