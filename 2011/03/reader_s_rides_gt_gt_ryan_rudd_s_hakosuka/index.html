March 1, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Before Ryan Rudd contacted us about Wayne Hamil's Salt Flat racer, he originally sent a photo of his 1972 Hakosuka. The car lives with Ryan in Utah, but as there's snow on the ground and coincidently salt, he does not drive it. And I don't blame him. It is rare to ever see a Hakosuka Skyline this clean.

Thanks Ryan!
