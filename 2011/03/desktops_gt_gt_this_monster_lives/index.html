Desktops&gt;&gt; This Monster Lives

March 26, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Breaking cover for the very first time, the rotary powered Team Need for Speed D-Mac AE86 made its public debut at the Irish launch of the latest Need for Speed title, Shift2 Unleashed. To celebrate the occassion of this long-awaited moment, we've put together a series of dektop images for your viewing pleasure.

I still can't quite believe how quickly this all came about. 

Although it is not completely finished according to D-Mac's & MCNSport's high standards, it was absolutely amazing to see and hear this car move under its own power for the first time. What an incredible noise it makes.

Along with its upcoming full and in-depth feature, we'll be bringing you the latest of the build updates as D-Mac puts the finishing touches to his magnum opus.

-

Paddy McGrath
