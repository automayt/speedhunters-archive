Video Roll&gt;&gt;nw Built January Meet

March 10, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rES-mRidg7w

It’s been less than 24 hours since our call out for you guys to share your local meets with us and we’ve already had a huge a response. As I started to dig through the inbox this morning, one thing that caught my eye was this video clip that David Green sent me featuring NW Built’s meet in Seattle, Washington. The notorious rainy weather didn’t stop some of the Northwest’s best from coming out.

Speedline Filmwerks did a fantastic job capturing the laid back atmosphere that all good meets should have.

There are also some seriously cool looking cars in here. Have a watch!

-Mike Garrett
