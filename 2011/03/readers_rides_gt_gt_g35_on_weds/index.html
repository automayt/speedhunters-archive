Readers Rides&gt;&gt;g35 On Weds

March 5, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

The post-R34 chassis Skylines might not get the same level of attention as their older brothers, but they are a very important part of the Skyline lineage. They can also look damn good when done right.

For example, we have Robin Har's G35 Coupe from Irvine, California. Robin's outfitted his car with a tasteful combination of mods, including a set of Weds Sport SA-55M wheels sized 19×9.5 in the front and 19×10.5 in the rear.

Well done sir!

-Mike Garrett
