News&gt;&gt; Kazama Auto D1 2jz S15

March 31, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Kazama over at Kazama Auto is putting the final touches on Tanaka's new D1-GP machine, a 2JZ powered S15 Silvia. There are already a few pictures of the car floating around on the company's website but it did look a bit plain without a full livery. The S15 is now at Art Factory getting the graphics applied and will eventually look something along what you see above. With Round 1 in Odaiba having been moved to the first weekend of June, following the earthquake, this year's opening round will be down in Autopolis on the 24th of this month.
