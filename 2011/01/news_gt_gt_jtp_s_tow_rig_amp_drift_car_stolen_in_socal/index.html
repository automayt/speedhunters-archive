News&gt;&gt;jtp&#039;s Tow Rig &amp; Drift Car Stolen In Socal

January 19, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Our good friend Justin Pawlak had some very disturbing news for us this morning – his truck and trailer had been stolen with his green FC RX7 drift car inside.

Here's Justins's description of the stolen items -

"Last night around 1am my 2004 chevy 2500hd with my 26' enclosed aztex
trailer were stolen.  inside the trailer was my green fc rx7, 2 18×11
ssr type f wheels, and 2 18×10.5 volk te37's.  Also in the trailer were a
craftsman tool box full of tools.  Please be on the look out for any
and all of these."

It was stolen from Maywood Street in the Eagle Rock area.

Here's a link to the photo of the truck and trailer – http://i6.photobucket.com/albums/y203/Justone1/IMG00142.jpg

Those of you in California – If you've seen anything that resembles this, please contact your local law enforcement immediately or contact Justin at 623-332-6766 or by email at jtpawlak (at) gmail.com.

Let's help Justin get his stuff back, and put the low-lifes responsible for this where they belong.

-Mike Garrett
