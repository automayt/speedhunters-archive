News&gt;&gt; Ts-1x Cr-z

January 11, 2011 Dino Dalle Carbonare

It might not be the Type-R version of the CR-Z everyone has been hoping for but the TS-1X that will be unveiled at the Tokyo Auto Salon this Friday is definitely something interesting. Built by Access Corporation, a Honda-owned subsidiary which manufacturers genuine Honda aftermarket parts, the TS-1X is fitted with new aftermarket parts that will boost its appearance and hopefully power too. Joining it will be a Modulo NSX and CR-Z sporting yet more upgrades that will become available through Japanese Honda dealers later in the year.

I wonder what else is in store for the CR-Z at TAS!

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
