Announcement&gt;&gt;modified Mag Reader Ride Submissions

January 11, 2011 TheSpeedhunters

Every year we put out our annual Reader’s Rides issue and this year what better way to advertise it then to post it on Speedhunters. This issue is dedicated to the cars that you guys (and gals) drive everyday, whether it's a beat up drift machine or a pristine street car, we are looking to show it off.

In order to have your car considered for a feature you need to follow these 5 simple rules:

1.Take photos of your car in HIGH RESOLUTION (at least 1mb in size) format. The nicer the photo the better.
2.Include a brief history of your car and why you chose it/drive it. You can add any details here that you think don't fit into the spec sheet.
3.Include a spec sheet of all the modifications done to your car in the follow format:
            Car (year and model)
            Engine Mods
            Wheels, Brakes & Suspension Mods
            Exterior Mods
            Interior Mods           
            Power output ( Just leave it blank if you don’t know)
4. Your full name and contact info.
5. Email your entries to readersrides (at) modified.com with the heading Readers Rides 2011.

-Modified Magazine
