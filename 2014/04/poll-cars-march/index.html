The Cars Of March

April 15, 2014 Mike Garrett

April is a huge month for Speedhunters as we roll at full speed into the new event season, and while we are already attacking it in a maximum fashion we want to take one last look back at March. More specifically, the feature cars of March. It was another month of fine machinery from mad street drift cars to historic race machines and everything in between, and now you guys are going to be tasked with picking the best of the bunch.

Here they are…

Hamana Benzes Exercise in Stance: The Hamana Benzes

Think long and hard, then make your vote below.
