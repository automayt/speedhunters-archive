ADRENALIN: The BMW Touring Car Story

October 14, 2014 Brad Lord

We’ve been treated to some pretty amazing automotive films over the course of 2014, and if the official trailer is anything to go by, ADRENALIN – The BMW Touring Car Story is definitely going to be added to that list.

Directed and produced by brothers Tim and Nick Hahne of German film making company Stereoscreen, the soon-to-be-released (by VoD and Blu-ray) documentary tells the story of BMW’s rich and successful touring car endeavours from the ’60s to the present day, through the legendary machines and the minds of past masters and modern heroes.

Take a look and I’m sure you’ll agree – this is going to be good!
