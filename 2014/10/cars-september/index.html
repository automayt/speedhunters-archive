The Cars Of September

October 3, 2014 Brad Lord

On the feature car front, September was definitely a diverse month. From American pro-touring racecars, to reimagined European classics, to a hypercar concept that never got the production green light and even a drift-spec kei truck. With those cars and pretty much everything else in between, there truly was something for everyone. And on that note, now’s the time when you get to pick your favourite.
