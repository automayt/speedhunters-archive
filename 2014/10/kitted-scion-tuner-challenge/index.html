Kitted Out For The Scion Tuner Challenge

October 15, 2014 TheSpeedhunters

As the 2014 Scion Tuner Challenge car construction phase nears its end, our retro IMSA-inspired FR-S is really starting to take shape. Having only been given 90 days to transform the car from showroom spec to SEMA Show standard, the build’s lead – Speedhunters’ Technical Editor, Keith Charvonia – and a small but dedicated crew have been putting in some big hours to realize the ambitious vision.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B0nrmT6OFYw

We gave you a look at Andy Blackmore Design’s concept art in the last update, and now with the release of another video update from Scion (filmed a few weeks back), you can see some of those ideas come to life – including fitting of the now-available-for-purchase KM4SH kit. Check it out above!
