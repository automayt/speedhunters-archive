Open October With A Sheer Joy Attack

September 30, 2014 Jonathan Moore

Over the last couple of months we’ve been keeping up a steady drip feed of goodies into the Speedhunters store for you to get your hands on. You’ve had a whole slew of ultra cool model kits, some great new colours of iPhone skins, that giant sticker set (I need a new car to sticker bomb now), Kanji windscreen vinyls and more. Now it’s time to turn our attention back to keeping you looking your best, with a new line of themed tees and hoodie – and of course another sticker. You can never have too many…

First up we have a brand new hoodie for you: this one a straight-up pull-over classic with kangaroo pockets at the front. You’ve been asking for them: we’ve listened, and here they are. These pull-over hoodies feature a quality flock Speedhunters logo across the chest, with an SH logo on the peak of the hood.

Round the back, you get a #MaximumAttack logo on the shoulder. I live in my Speedhunters hoodies, so I’m super pleased there’s another one to add to the collection!

Our three new T-shirts utilise top quality, fitted American Apparel stock, and all feature the Joy Of Machine graphics on the front.

The turquoise shirt is a way to keep bright as the evenings grow darker in the northern hemisphere at least – and to keep up with the sun if you’re lucky enough to be down south.

The heather grey and black shirts come with easter egg messages on the inner hem.

On the shoulder, all the shirts feature a smaller Joy Of Machine logo and graphical device. They’re available in mens’ cuts and come up slim, so upsize for a baggy fit.

Last but not least, we’ve got a new bumper sticker to add to your collection. This Joy Of Machine decal measures nine by three inches (225mm x 70mm), and is the perfect adornment for your car. Or someone else’s if you’re feeling particularly generous. There are more to come, of course. The next one may well be rocket-powered… watch this space!

So, pull it on, wear it or stick it: show off your commitment to the Joy Of Machine with one of our new autumn lines. Head over to the Speedhunters store: they’re all available right now.
