The Cars Of July

August 4, 2014 Brad Lord

On the feature car front, July 2014 will was absolutely filled to the brim. The Scandinavian summer Gatebil season helped the cause, of course, but from Europe, to the USA, to Japan, global car culture was well and truly covered through full features and spotlights. But now that August has arrived it’s time to look back at the month that was July, and for you to pick your favourites. Revisit the features below and then state your case in the comments sections at the bottom of the page.

In no particular order, July’s contenders are…

Check Shop Porsche 911 GT3 – Track Bound: The Check Shop 911 GT3

Daruma Toyota Celica – Perseverance First: The Daruma Celica

Valtonen SR20-Powered Mazda RX-7 – Rage Within The Machine: The Valtonen RX-7

TS-Racing Audi RS2 – The Wagon Warrior: An Audi RS2… Evolved

SRT-Powered Dodge Coronet – Hybrid Technology? Meet The Coroner

Stateside Nissan Skyline Hakosuka – A Hakosuka In The Hills

SR20-Powered KE70 Toyota Corolla – Heart & Soul: An SR20-Swapped KE70 Corolla

The Galaxian – Galaxian: Japan Reinvents The Hot Rod

Ex-DTM E30 BMW M3 – Old DTM Cars Don’t Die… They Get Faster

The Volvo Super Amazon – The AWD, Turbo V8, Volvo Super Amazon

Ford Shoebox Kustom – Sweet Brown: Kustom Refined & Mild Defined

’33 Chevy Hot Rod – ’33 Chevy Master Class: From Barn Find To Bangin’ Frame

918-Inspired Porsche Cayman – A Porsche Cayman With 918 Aspirations

Fail Crew V8 Nissan Silvia S13 – From Russia With Roar: The Fail Crew S13

Old School Toyota Minitruck – Minitruckin’ The Old School Way

Gatebil BNR34 Nissan Skyline GT-R, The Stuff GT-R Dreams Are Made Of

Camaro-Faced Nissan 180SX – Get Funky: Nissan 180SX Meets Chevy Camaro

M3-Inspired BMW Wagon – Planned & Slammed: A Phantom Wagon

Chevy V8-Powered Mercedes-Benz – Old Benz, New Tricks: Slammed With A Small Block

V8-Powered Mitsubishi Lancer Evo – Big-Boost Chevy V8 Meets Mitsubishi Evo

BMW V10-Powered Volvo 282 – Looks Like A Volvo, Pulls Like A V10 BMW, Brakes Like A Porsche

700hp Chevy Wheelie Van – Boogie Van: Engine In The Back, Wheels In The Air
