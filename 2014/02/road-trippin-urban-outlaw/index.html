Road Trippin&#039; With The Urban Outlaw

February 7, 2014 Brad Lord

With a downtown LA garage full of retro cool Porsches just begging to be driven hard, Magnus Walker is spoilt for choice when Southern California’s finest canyon roads beckon. But when the Urban Outlaw journeyed to the East Coast of the United States last October on his ‘Southern Charm & Hospitality Road Trip’, there was only one car he wanted to be behind the wheel of: his ’71 911T ‘277’ race car.

While Magnus hit up some of East Carolina’s finest and most picturesque driving roads and turned some laps at VIRginia International Speedway, the guys at eGarage were along for the ride, wrapping up the experience with this very nicely executed little clip that’s well worth six minutes of your day.
