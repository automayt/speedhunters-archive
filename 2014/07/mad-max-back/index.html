Mad Max Is Back

July 31, 2014 Mike Garrett

With all the remakes, reboots and sequels out there today, it seems that original movie ideas are hard to come by. If there was, however, one film franchise that seems ripe for a comeback it would be Mad Max. Last week at Comic-Con in San Diego, a trailer for 2015’s Mad Max: Fury Road was released, and I’ve got to say that I’m pretty excited for this. While I’m generally skeptical of remakes, this film is being directed by the original Mad Max director George Miller. And from what the trailer shows us it seems to perfectly capture the feel of the originals – albeit with a modern twist.

Is anyone else looking forward to this?
