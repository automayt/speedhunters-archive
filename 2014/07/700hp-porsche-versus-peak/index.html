A 700hp Porsche Versus The Peak

July 18, 2014 Brad Lord

One of the world’s most challenging motorsport playgrounds also happens to be one of the most visually spectacular, so every year after the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb, beautiful still and moving pictures emerge. Earlier in the month Larry shared with us his captures from the 2014 running of the PPIHC, but now – on the release of an amazing short film – we have to show you what director extraordinaire Will Roegge got up to at the very same event. With Pikes Peak legend Jeff Zwart and his BBI Autosport-prepared 700hp Porsche 911 GT3 Cup Turbo Hill Climb Special as the subject, this has to be one of the finest films ever made on the mountain. For the full effect make sure you select glorious 4K (Ultra HD) for playback.
