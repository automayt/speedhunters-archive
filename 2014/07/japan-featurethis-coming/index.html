Japan! #FeatureThis Is Coming For You!

July 3, 2014 TheSpeedhunters

The second round of the 2014 Formula Drift Asia season is happening at Fuji Speedway in Japan this coming weekend, and we’re gearing up to run another Speedhunters #FeatureThis activation at the Offset Kings car showcase running in tandem with the main event on Sunday (July 6).

Organised by the crew at Fatlace, Offset Kings Japan follows on from the successful Slammed Society Japan event last year and is open to wide variety of vehicles spanning many automotive genres. If you’re planning on taking part or heading along, you’ll find everything you need to know here.

For #FeatureThis, we’ll be checking out all the cars taking part in the show and then picking our favorite for an on-the-spot Speedhunters photoshoot and upcoming feature on the site. Speedhunters’ Taryn Croucher and Peter Kelly will be at FSW on Sunday, so if you see them shooting, make sure you say hi!
