The Cars Of November Revisited

December 2, 2014 Brad Lord

Having the annual SEMA Show to kick off proceedings, November was always destined to be big on the feature car front. And with more than a few high-profile Vegas-debuting machines in the mix – plus a diverse line-up of modified cars from elsewhere around the world – the second to last month of 2014 had it all. Now it’s time for you to pick your favourite…
