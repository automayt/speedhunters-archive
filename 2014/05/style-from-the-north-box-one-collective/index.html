Style From The North

May 12, 2014 Mike Garrett

There are a lot of automotive filmmakers out there today producing some great stuff, and the level of creativity among them is constantly on the rise. One whose work we’ve been enjoying lately is Box One Collective based out of Vancouver, BC. The Canadian city is a bit of a hidden gem when it comes to to car culture, and these four Box One productions certainly prove that.

Among the short films Box One has produced is this profile of a Porsche 968 and its owner.

Here’s another that features a radical X8 Toyota Cressida built by the guys at Serial Nine. Like many of the BoxOne films, this one’s got a very street-heavy feel.

That same underground vibe can be felt in this look at a tough-looking Nissan Silvia S14.

Then there’s Box One’s most well known film: Night Run, which takes lots of inspiration from the movie Drive. Never before has going out and getting some gas felt so cool.

Keep up the good work guys!
