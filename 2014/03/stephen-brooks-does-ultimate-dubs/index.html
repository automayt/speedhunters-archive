Stephen Brooks Does Ultimate Dubs

March 27, 2014 Paddy McGrath

I don’t think any UK show coverage is complete without a Stephen Brooks video. It’s certainly a nice way to bring our Ultimate Dubs coverage to a close for another year.

Speaking to Stephen on the day, he was using the show as an excuse to put a Canon 1DC and its 4k resolution to test before he heads to Formula D next week. He’s been quietly playing down this video as nothing more than just having some fun and trying new things, but it seems that his version of ‘messing about’ is better than the rest of our attempts at trying hard. I’m quite envious!

I’m sure that some of you will appreciate the graphics which explain what details make particular cars special, while offering a little bit more information to satisfy curiosity too.
