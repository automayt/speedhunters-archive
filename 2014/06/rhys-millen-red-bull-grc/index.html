Wild In The Streets: Rhys Millen Cuts Loose In The Capital

June 20, 2014 Brad Lord

Imagine the possibilities… a Presidential-style police motorcade, a 600hp Supercar-spec rallycross machine, and closed-off city streets at your disposal. What would you do? If you’re thinking ‘rip it up’ or something along similar lines, you’ll probably want to check out this clip from Red Bull Global Rallycross filmed in Washington, D.C. and starring motorsports all rounder Rhys Millen.

Staged it might be, but that doesn’t take away anything from the spectacle – Rhys unleashing the full four-wheel drive might of his RMR GRC Hyundai Veloster Turbo, and sliding it by some of the USA’s most iconic architecture, including the Washington Monument and the Capitol Building. His final destination is the RFK Stadium where GRC’s third round is happening this weekend. Check it out!
