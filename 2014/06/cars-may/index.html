The Cars Of May

June 2, 2014 Mike Garrett

Welcome to June everyone, which for many people around the world means the coming of summer and the heart of the event season. We’ve got another big month planned, but before we really dive into June let’s take a look back at the cars of May.

We’ve listed the month’s feature cars below, so check out the ones you might have missed and let us know your favorite below. Here they are…

Lexon Japan LFA: The Lexus Screamer

Retro ’99 Miata: Carve The Canyons, Stir The Soul: The Perfect Miata 

The Players’ E36 BMWs: Playing a Joker: The Winning Hand

Daddy Motorworks Fairlady Z: S30 + 2JZ + Airbags = A New Way To Z 

JTune Automotive S2000: Porsche Hunting In A Honda

Fit Kustoms ’57 Nomad: 日本カスタム: The Other JDM

Magnus Walker’s Porsche 911 SC: How To Build An Everyday Outlaw

Top Secret R35 GT-R: The Competizione R Evolves

Renault 5 Turbo: Five Alive: The Volcanic Hot Hatch

Monster Sport Super 86: The 86 That So Isn’t An 86

Accuair Audi S7: Cruisin’ The Family Supercar

Endless R32 Skyline GT-R: 1170HP For The Street

We are still working out some kinks with our polling system, but in the meantime you can shout out your pick in the comments section and tell your fellow Speedhunters why you chose it.
