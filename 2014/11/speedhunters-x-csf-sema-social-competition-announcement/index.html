It&#039;s Competition Time: Win With CSF Cooling At SEMA!

November 4, 2014 TheSpeedhunters

This year at the SEMA Show we’ve teamed up with our partners at CSF Radiators to give you the chance to WIN some exciting prizes, including four advance copies of our new Speedhunters Feature Cars.01 art book. The 4th volume in our art book series, Feature Cars.01 is huge – literally. It contains a whopping 450 pages of our very best Feature Cars photography, and it’s set to be released very soon – but this is your only chance to get your hands on a copy before everyone else.

The best part about it? You don’t even need to be at the SEMA Show to be in to win. We’ll be choosing three winners at SEMA and one online winner, so anyone can enter. Here’s how it’s going to work:

If you’re at the SEMA Show:

  • Find the CSF Radiators booth (booth 21509 in the Central Hall) 
  • Take a photo of the BBI/CSF/Liberty Walk Porsche 997 Turbo at the CSF Radiators booth and upload it to *Instagram with the hashtag #CSFradiators
  • You’ll automatically go into the draw to win a daily prize pack including a bunch of CSF goodies and one of the very first copies of the all new Speedhunters Feature Cars.01 book!
  • Make sure to follow @CSF_Radiators on Instagram where they’ll be announcing the winners at **3:00pm PST each day.

If you’re not at the SEMA Show:

  • Find @CSF_Radiators on Instagram and locate the promo image featuring the BBI/CSF/Liberty Walk Porsche 997 Turbo
  • Repost this promo image to your *Instagram account along with the hashtag #CSFradiators
  • You’ll automatically go into the draw to win a daily prize pack including a bunch of CSF goodies and one of the very first copies of the all new Speedhunters Feature Cars.01 book – before it goes on sale!
  • Make sure to follow @CSF_Radiators on Instagram where they’ll be announcing the winner at ***3pm PST on the 7th of November.

CSF have some awesome goodies to give away as well, so whether you’re able to make it to SEMA in person or not, make sure to enter to be in to win!

Head on over to the CSF Radiators Instagram account and find the competition image to repost, or head on over to the CSF booth in person to check out the incredible BBI/CSF Liberty Walk Porsche 997 Turbo on display, and get ‘graming!
