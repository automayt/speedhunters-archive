Looping The C1 At Warp Speed

November 10, 2014 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Video games aside, have you ever wondered what it looks and feels like to take a lap of Tokyo’s C1 loop? I’ve tried to give you different glimpses of it in the past, and you may remember the lap I did with Project GT-R last year. But a few weeks back, while I had the new BMW M3 for a short test drive, I thought I’d suction-mount a GoPro to the windscreen and go for a blast. I think that what I came up with looks pretty cool, so I thought I’d share.

If you know your way around Tokyo, you’ll probably notice that the loop I did is a little bit longer than the actual C1. That’s because I took off from Tatsumi PA, used part of Route 9 to connect to the C1 and then jumped on Rainbow Bridge to head back to the PA.

Anyway, let me know what you think! I always find driving the C1 strangely calming. Sometimes I’ll do three or four loops just for the hell of it, and it always puts a smile on my face. Switch to 1080p, hit play and enjoy…
