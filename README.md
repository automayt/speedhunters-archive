# Speedhunters Archive

A static archive of [Speedhunters.com](https://www.speedhunters.com), the legendary automotive culture and photography magazine that ran from 2008 to 2025.

## What's here

- **15,751 articles** spanning 17 years of car culture coverage
- **250 writers** including Larry Chen, Dino Dalle Carbonare, Paddy McGrath, and many more
- Coverage of drifting, time attack, car shows, builds, motorsport, and street culture worldwide
- Photography-first editorial content with images served from the original S3 CDN

## About

Speedhunters was founded in 2008 as a collaboration between EA Games and several automotive photographers and journalists. It grew into one of the most respected automotive culture publications on the internet, known for its stunning photography and deep coverage of car scenes around the world.

When the site went offline, this archive was created from a web crawl to preserve the content. Article text is hosted here as static HTML; images are still served from their original AWS S3 URLs.

## Technical

This static site was generated from crawled HTML using a Python-based parser and Jinja2 template engine. The source generator is not included in this repository.
