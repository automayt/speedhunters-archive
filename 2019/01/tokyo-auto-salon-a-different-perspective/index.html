Tokyo Auto Salon: A Different Perspective

January 22, 2019 Dino Dalle Carbonare

We’re always trying to come up with different ways in which to share the car culture we hunt out around the world.

For this year’s Tokyo Auto Salon we wanted to add to our usual in-depth coverage by creating a film to showcase this massive event through our eyes.

We once again called Phil Boquida on board to follow me around, stopping off at the cars that stood out the most to us Speedhunters. More to the point, we wanted to introduce a new dimension to our coverage, one we know you’ll like. This is one is a little bit longer that what we’ve put together in the past and we hope you enjoy  experiencing TAS from a different perspective.
