#SHSummerOfSwag: Win A KW V3 Coilover Kit!

May 25, 2015 Taryn Croucher

Summer is just around the corner, and if you’re anything like us here at Speedhunters, that means it’s time to put those finishing touches on your beloved project car. Whether you’re looking for an upgrade in handling or a lower ride height, an improved cooling system, new safety gear or something to get your car looking pristine, we’ve got you covered with our Speedhunters Summer of Swag!

We’ve teamed up with our fantastic partners to bring you some incredible giveaways over the next couple of months, starting right now. Want to enter? Keep reading!

When it comes to high quality aftermarket performance-oriented suspension components, the KW name doesn’t need an introduction. Over the last 20 years, KW has grown from a small shop in Germany to a global brand with numerous race wins and championships. This week and this week only, our friends at KW Suspension are going to be giving away one brand new Version 3 coilover kit to one very lucky Speedhunters reader…

Can you imagine the feeling of sheer joy as you unbox your very own set of KW coilovers? This could be you!

Want to be in to win? Here’s how to enter:

  • Find @thespeedhunters on Instagram or @SPEEDHUNTERS on Twitter and locate the KW Competition promo image – you’ll know it when you see it
  • Repost this promo image to your Instagram or Twitter account along with the hashtag #SHSummerOfSwag
  • Include the make and model of your car, and tell us WHY you want to win a set of KW V3 Coilovers!
  • Make sure to follow @kw_suspension on Instagram and @KWautomotive on Twitter where they’ll be announcing the winner next Wednesday 3rd June at 9.00am PST 

The competition starts NOW – check our Instagram or Twitter account, find the competition image and get posting!
