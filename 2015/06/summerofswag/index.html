#SHSummerOfSwag: Win CSF Goodies For Your Car!

June 8, 2015 Taryn Croucher

If you missed out on our last competition as part of our Speedhunters Summer of Swag, don’t panic! This week we’ve got some more fantastic goodies to give away courtesy of our partners over at CSF Cooling. If you’re in need of a new cooling system for your car, CSF have you covered! Keep on reading to find out how you can win…

The team at CSF produce some of the most advanced, efficient and reliable cooling systems available in today’s market, and they’ve actually been around for more than 60 years, as we discovered while putting together a retrospective feature last year. By this time next week we’ll have gifted one lucky Speedhunters reader with their very own CSF high performance cooling system for their car! But that’s not all…

We’ve also got some awesome spot prizes to give away to three lucky Scion FR-S/Subaru BRZ/Toyota 86 owners. These all-aluminum CSF filler necks feature a high pressure cap, and they’re stronger and a lot more functional than the stock OEM plastic part which they replace. So if you’re an FR-S, BRZ or 86 owner, make sure to enter as these items are model-specific for your cars.

Want to be in to win? Here’s how to enter:

  • Find @csf_radiators on Instagram or @CSFrace on Twitter and locate the CSF Competition promo image – you’ll know it when you see it. (We’ll also be posting the image on our social channels.)
  • Repost this promo image to your Instagram or Twitter account along with the hashtag #SHSummerOfSwag
  • Include the make and model of your car, and tell us WHY you want to win a high performance cooling system for your car!
  • Make sure to follow @csf_radiators on Instagram or @CSFrace on Twitter where they’ll be announcing the winner next Wednesday 17th June at 8.00am PST 

The competition starts NOW – check CSF’s Instagram or Twitter account, find the competition image and get posting!
