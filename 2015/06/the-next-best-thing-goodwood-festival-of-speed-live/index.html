The Next Best Thing: Goodwood Festival of Speed - Live

June 25, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

With its unmatched line-up of significant race cars both past and present, the Goodwood Festival of Speed is one of the most anticipated events on the Speedhunting calendar. Our UK man on the ground, Mr. Jonathan Moore, will be spending the next few days at Goodwood House, soaking up everything this world-famous event has to offer in order to bring you a serving of spectacular coverage next week. Look out for a special video from new Speedhunters recruit Jason Laroza too…

We can’t all be in the West Sussex countryside of course, but right now you can catch every bit of the action from #FOS – including the brand new ‘Catch My Drift’ category, featuring Ken Block, Mad Mike Whiddett and others – for free and as it happens via the official high definition livestream. It’s the next best thing to being there!

Press play above to launch the YouTube livestream, or check it out on Goodwood’s Dailymotion channel.

Between now and Sunday the live action kicks off every morning at 8.30am BST (12:30am PST) – or just rewind at your leisure.
