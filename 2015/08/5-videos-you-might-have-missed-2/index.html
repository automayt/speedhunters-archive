5 Videos You Might Have Missed...

August 16, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

Just before we jump into a new week of Speedhunting, here’s five video clips from the past week or so that we think are worth a look if you haven’t checked them out already…

Porsche 918 Spyder: Behind The Scenes Production

Only 918 examples of Porsche’s amazing 918 Spyder were built during the plug-in hybrid sportscar’s production period, and in this behind-the-scenes video from the German automaker you get a taste for how the exclusive machines came together. No music, no commentary and no effects – just raw footage captured from inside the Porsche 918’s 4000-square-metre ‘manufactory’ as some of the 100 hand-selected employees chosen to build the special model work away.

Matt Powers’ 360-Degree Drift

Last year, Will Roegge used Larry’s Ole Orange Bang 240Z to capture ex-FD driver Matt Powers pulling off a mid-drift 360-degree spin. If you liked that, then you’re going to like this new clip from GoPro, which shows Matt getting up to his old tricks, but this time at Sonoma Raceway and with the full-circle action caught from multiple angles – including inside the car.

BMW Concept M4 GTS

One year on from the launch of the M4 Coupe, BMW – or more specifically its M Division – is giving us a preview of a high performance road/track variant: the BMW Concept M4 GTS. According to the German automaker, the Concept M4 GTS will follow in the footsteps of previous special BMW M3 special models, including the 1988 M3 Evolution, 2003 M3 CSL and 2010 M3 GTS among others. Of the new car, BMW M Division’s President Frank van Meel says, “Despite its outstanding track ability, it is still fully road-legal. This is racing technology for the road in the truest sense.” Some of the Concept M4 GTS’s features include staggered 19×9.5-inch (front) and 20×10.5-inch (rear) forged wheels, water injection for its turbocharged six-cylinder engine, an expansive use of carbon fibre and OLED (organic light-emitting diodes) rear lights. You can read more about the car here.

From Riga With Love

There are drift event videos, and then there is this… For the official video coverage of Drift Allstars’ Round 3 event held in Latvia, the guys at Aatomotion did something a little different: mix traditional on-track action with the storyline of a Hollywood blockbuster. High speed sideways drifts courtesy of Luke Fink, a Hummer and angry guys with automatic weapons – From Riga With Love has it all.

1991 Mazda RX-7 IMSA GTO In-Car

Lastly this week, we have a clip from Mazda that was filmed ahead of this weekend’s Rolex Monterey Motorsports Reunion (of which Mike will be bringing us coverage this week). It’s a video from within the 1991 Mazda RX-7 IMSA GTO driven by Jeremy Barnes, and for rotary fans the sound of the R26B 4-rotor motor alone should be enough of a reason to hit play. You can also check out an interactive 360-video from the same car, similar vision from a 1992 Mazda RX-792P, plus in-car from a 1990 Mazda 787 – all from the same event.
