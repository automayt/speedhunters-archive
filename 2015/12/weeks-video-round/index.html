This Week&#039;s Video Round-Up

December 6, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

Yes, it’s time again to take a quick look at some of our favorite video clips from around the net that came to our attention over the past week.

Let’s kick things off with a new short film from Petrolicious entitled 7 Minutes Of Pure Ferrari 250 GT0 Hillclimb Bliss… 

The concept is simple: Derek Hill behind the wheel of a Ferrari 250 GTO on a mountain road. No narration, no distracting background music – just pure, unadulterated driving in one of the most iconic performance machines of all time. Turn up the volume and press play to hear its V12 sing!

Next up, it’s a promotional video for Japan’s Fukushima Prefecture, which since the 2011 earthquake and subsequent tsunami and nuclear powerplant disaster has seen many less tourists travelling to the picturesque region of the country. If cars – or specifically drifting is your thing – Ebisu Circuit is one very good reason to visit though, and in this clip hosted by Power Vehicles, you get a real sense of what it’s all about and what you can do there.

We don’t need to tell you how wicked a machine the Dodge Charger SRT Hellcat is, but this video goes a long way in showing just how much potential is waiting to be unlocked with what are essentially bolt-on modifications. Built by High Horse Performance, an 18psi upper pulley, ATI lower pulley, cat-less mid-pipes and a Diablosport CMR tune is pretty much all it took to bring power up to 902hp, while a 3:09 final and driveshaft upgrade from The Driveshaft Shop gets it to the ground for 9-second quarters.

Finally this week we have the official video from the 3rd annual StanceNation Japan G Edition Tokyo event. If you’ve been digging Dino’s coverage from this event, you’re going to enjoy this. Look out for a special cameo appearance from the man himself too…
