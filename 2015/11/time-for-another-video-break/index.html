Time For Another Video Break...

November 22, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

We’re very close to kicking off a new week of Speedhunting, which means it’s time to take a quick look back at some of the videos we’ve been watching over the past seven days.

First up, we’ve got a new clip from the guys at Rotiform, in which an RWB Porsche 964 plays the starring role…

Through vision and music, The Roads Less Traveled – LV to LA, tells the story of RWB Atlanta returning to Los Angeles from a week spent on display at the 2015 SEMA Show. Who wouldn’t want to make that drive in a Nakai-san built 964?

From a sweet edit to raw sound and vision – next it’s an opportunity to ride onboard the legendary Pennzoil Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 GT500 around Fuji Speedway. You could say that GT500-spec machines were the ultimate embodiment of the R34 GT-R, and with Nismo works driver Alex Buncombe at the wheel, it doesn’t get much better. Turn up your speakers!

360-degree cameras provide us with a new POV way to see things, and when it’s motorsport-related the results are always spectacular. In this clip from Scion Racing, the camera mounted on the roof of the Papadakis Racing Scion tC is in your hands as Fredric Aasbø attacks Irwindale Speedway. The GReddy Racing/Ken Gushi version can be found here.

Finally this week, it’s the LaFerrari versus Porsche 918 versus McLaren P1 group track thrash to end all group track thrashes – Chris Harris, with Tiff Needell and Marino Franchitti see to that. Filmed at Autódromo Internacional do Algarve in Portimão, Portugal, this clip has it all. 

Watched a cool automotive video online this week? Share it with us in the comments section below!
