What We&#039;ve Been Watching This Week...

November 1, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

A new week of Speedhunting is almost upon us, and with the annual SEMA Show just a couple of days away, it’s going to be a big one – that’s guaranteed. But right now it’s time to take a quick look at a few videos we’ve watched this week…

First up, it’s a new instalment in the Z Dream build: Episode 3: Crunch Time. The Datsun 240Z (aka FuguZ) owned by Sung Kang (Fast & Furious), is one car we’re looking forward to checking out complete in Las Vegas, but in the third clip from the video series, it’s all about reassembly at GReddy HQ.

A couple of weeks back we shared Keep Drifting Fun’s very cool Final Bout II documentary; this week we’ve got another KDF-produced short film from the unique event, as shot for Hoonigan. This is grassroots drifting at its best.

Ford Performance’s Rebirth of an Icon behind-the-scenes video series is up to Episode 5: Arctic Extremes, and as the name suggests, this time around the new Focus RS is put through its paces during rigorous winter testing in Sweden. If you need to catch up, you can find Episode 3: Hitting the Road and Episode 4: Test-track Trials by following the links.

Finally this week we have something a little, er… different. The name AMS Performance is best known for crazy-fast Nissan GT-R builds, but the Illinois-based tuner can turn its hand to anything with four wheels – even a hearse it seems. Because, who wouldn’t want to ride in a 1300hp Chevy Caprice that’s now proven its 9-second worth on the strip? The secret – a 370ci LQ4 V8 with twin Garrett GTW3884R turbochargers…
