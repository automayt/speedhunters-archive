The Best Way To Rally?

January 21, 2015 Dino Dalle Carbonare

We know what you’re thinking and you’re probably right; this is far from the fastest way to rally around any special stage, let alone the wet, icy and slippery stages above Monte Carlo. But for a skilled driver, trying to keep a rear-engined supercar like the 997 Porsche GT3 under control in conditions like this must be a ton of fun.

Hit play and check out French driving legend François Delecour feathering the accelerator and using the Porsche’s rear-biased momentum to steer it around some really tight and treacherous mountain corners. We’re thinking about putting this video on loop and having it play 24/7 in the office – purely for the aural pleasure alone. Are we the only ones that would love to see more Porsches back in rallies?
