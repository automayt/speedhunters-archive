Need for Speed: Five Ways To Play

September 3, 2015 TheSpeedhunters

With every passing week we’re getting closer to the launch of the new Need for Speed on November 3, 2015. Over the past couple of months we’ve shown you everything from the Official Trailer to a look at how the in-game cars are made, to the behind-the-scenes steps involved in capturing reference smoke, and more. Now there’s a new video to check out…

This time around it’s all about gameplay, specifically what it takes to make it on the streets of Ventura Bay and progress through the game.

As you’ll see by watching the clip above, money earned from competing in a range of different events including Drift Train, Gymkhana and Sprint Race allows you to build up your garage and modify its contents, which in turn will help increase your rep. Score big in both these aspects and you’ll win big in the game.
