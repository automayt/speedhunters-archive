News&gt;&gt; Ferrari F12 Berlinetta Unveiled

February 29, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Ferrari has today released a few images of the 599 GTB Fiorano replacement ahead of its Geneva Motor Show unveil. The all-new F12berlinetta is the fastest and most powerful Ferrari ever, boasting a massive 729 HP from its 6.3L V12. The car is packed with technology from an all-aluminum spaceframe chassis, to an advanced dual-clutch transaxle transmission capable of coping with the 508 lb/ft the engine also develops. While there are a few styling cues borrowed from the FF the F12berlinetta has a fresh new style mixing beautiful flowing lines with aggressive details. Can’t wait so see more detailed images emerge next week from Geneva.

Ferrari

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
