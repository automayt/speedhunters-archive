Video Roll&gt;&gt; Ken Block, A Fiesta &amp; A Bison Ranch

February 23, 2012 Charles Kha

This year’s Rally in the 100 Acre Wood kicks off tomorrow in Missouri, and a certain Ken Block will be hoping to make it six wins from six attempts. In the lead up to the event, the Monster World Rally Team paid a visit to the SayersBrook Bison Ranch to get some testing under their belts. Check out the video to see Block and his Fiesta in action.
