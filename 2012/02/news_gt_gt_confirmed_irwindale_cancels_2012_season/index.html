News&gt;&gt; Confirmed: Irwindale Cancels 2012 Season

February 13, 2012 Charles Kha

Over the weekend there were numerous reports speculating that California's Irwindale Speedway had been closed. Sadly, those rumours appear to be true. This afternoon, the management of Irwindale Speedway released a one sentence statement confirming that the 2012 racing season has been cancelled. No further details regarding the long term future of the famous 1/2- and 1/3-mile oval have been released.

- Charles Kha
