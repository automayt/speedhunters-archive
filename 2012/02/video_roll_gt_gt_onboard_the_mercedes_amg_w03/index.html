Video Roll&gt;&gt; Onboard The Mercedes Amg W03

February 17, 2012 Charles Kha

Yesterday we shared a short spy video on our Facebook page showing the shakedown of the Mercedes AMG W03. Mercedes – who have yet to officially unveil their new F1 challenger – were undertaking a promotional filming day at Silverstone, with drivers Rosberg and Schumacher covering 100km. The team have today released this neat POV video with Nico behind the wheel.
