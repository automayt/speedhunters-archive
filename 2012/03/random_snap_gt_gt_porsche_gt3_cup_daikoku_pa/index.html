Random Snap&gt;&gt; Porsche Gt3 Cup @ Daikoku Pa

March 14, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

I was down at Daikoku PA last night with a few friends of mine when I heard the unmistakable noise of a close to unsilenced Porsche flat-six. Now we all know that the Japanese aren't too fussed about driving highly tuned, stripped-out, track-prepped cars on the street but driving a fully-fledged race car on the road is plain crazy! Or is it? The owner and his buddy stopped for a quick drink, climbed back into the 996 contorting over the roll cage and were off again onto the Wangan. If you could get away with it, what race car would you drive on the street? The new R35 GT-R GT3 might make a nice C1 racer!

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
