Video Roll&gt;&gt; Under Suzuki Behind The Scenes

March 15, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

We’ve all seen Under Suzuki in action at the Battle Evome series, not to mention WTAC last year, but it’s hard to fully understand the sacrifices and hard work a privateer like him needs to make in order to compete at this level. The guys from Video Option always follow Suzuki-san around at events and, on this particular video released the other day on Advan Australia’s YouTube channel, we can see what everyone’s favorite time attack driver gets up the night before a big event. Sleep is something him and Takemura-san of Scorch Racing don’t really get much of!
