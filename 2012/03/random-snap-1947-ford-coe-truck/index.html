Random Snap&gt;&gt; 1947 Ford Coe Truck

March 23, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Central Tokyo is always a great area to do a bit of car spotting and on top of all the modern high end cars roaming around, you always end up coming across something that makes you smile. The other day, while walking towards my favorite coffee shop, I spotted this 1947 Ford COE truck. I actually saw this very truck back in 2010 at the Mooneyes Hot Rod & Custom Show in Yokohama. Certainly not your everyday sighting in Japan!

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
