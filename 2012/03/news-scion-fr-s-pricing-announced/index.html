News&gt;&gt; Scion Fr-s Pricing Announced

March 21, 2012 Mike Garrett

Is the wait finally over? We’ve known the pricing for the Toyota 86 in many parts of the world for a while now, and it looks like pricing info for the USDM Scion FR-S has surfaced. According to info posted on FT86 Club, the FR-S with a six speed manual trans will go for $24,200 + destination charges (roughly $1000 or so). The automatic version will set buyers back $25,300 + destination.

Pricing info for this car has been highly anticipated, so we are curious to know what you guys think. How many of our US-based readers will be ready to pick one up when they hit dealers? How about readers in other countries? How many are holding out for Subaru BRZ pricing?

Sound off below.

-Mike Garrett
