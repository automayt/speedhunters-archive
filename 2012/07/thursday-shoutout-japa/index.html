Thursday Shoutout : Japan Edition

July 12, 2012 Mike Garrett

Since the beginning Speedhunters has looked to the wonderful country of Japan as a source for so much great content, but up until now we’ve felt that our Japanese readers were left out of our community activity because of the language barrier. With this in mind I (with the assistance of my wife) worked up a call-out message asking our friends from Japan to submit their machines to the featurethis@dev.speedhunters.com mailbox. The response has been tremendous and we figured we would dedicate this week’s edition of the Thursday Shoutout to our first batch of submissions from Japan. Here they are.
