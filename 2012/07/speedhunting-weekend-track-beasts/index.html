Speedhunting Weekend // Track Beasts

July 27, 2012 Rob Bullough

And we’re back. The ninth instalment Speedhunting Weekend is about to begin.

With half of the Speedhunters crew covering the 24 hours of madness currently about to ignite over at Spa Francorchamps and the Olympics now moments away from exploding out of half the world’s telly boxes, we would like to dedicate this week’s theme to performance. We’re looking for cars which sweat, ache & ooze racecar. We’re looking for machines which simply belong to the track.

If you haven’t followed this feature before, the idea is that you go out and take pictures of your’s, your friend’s or anybody else’s cars and post them up to Twitter or Instagram along with the hashtag #SpeedhuntingWKND. At the end of the weekend, we will choose our favourites and award them with super special Speedhunters stickers and their work featured on the website.

All clear? Great. Then let us begin. SpeedhuntingWKND Round 09 is now officially open, this week’s theme: “Track Beasts”.

Enjoy.
