Slow Motion Sliding, Australian Style

July 16, 2012 Jonathan Moore

Berry Brothers Motorsport are running a pair of Mitsubishi Lancer Evos (a IV and a VI) in this year’s Bosch Australian Rally Championship, and have released this promo clip to show off their cars in action.

A Red Epic camera was used, with a lot of the video recorded at 300 frames per second, so the slow-mo footage lives up to the camera’s name. The Berry Brothers, Nathan and Dane, are taking part in a limited calendar of events this year after stepping up from the Australian Junior Challenge: next up for them is the Scouts Rally SA at the end of this month.
