Video Roll&gt;&gt;dakar 2012 - The Final Stage

January 15, 2012 TheSpeedhunters

The 2012 Dakar Rally is now in the books, with Stephane Peterhansel holding on for the overall win in his Mini. Nani Roma and Giniel de Villiers take second and third respectively. Robby Gordon ended up in fifth overall after taking the win on the final stage.
