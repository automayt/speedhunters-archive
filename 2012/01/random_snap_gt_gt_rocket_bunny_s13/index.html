Random Snap&gt;&gt; Rocket Bunny S13

January 3, 2012 Ross I&#039;Anson

The Nissan S13 was a recipe that simply worked. Fairly powerful in stock trim, rear wheel drive and with seemingly endless potential. Here in the UK, it spawned hundreds of drift cars and started many a driver's career. When we were asked to recall our favourite example for our S-Chassis Festival, Anthony Scott's Rocket Bunny Silvia immediately came to mind. It was the first Rocket Bunny S13 in Europe and packed a 500hp RB30 motor. It's currently undergoing a major refresh for 2012, so expect to see a lot more of this Rocket Bunny S13 throughout the year. 

Ross.
