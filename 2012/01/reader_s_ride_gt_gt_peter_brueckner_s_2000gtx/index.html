Reader&#039;s Ride&gt;&gt; Peter Brueckner&#039;s 2000gtx

January 19, 2012 Charles Kha

Speedhunters reader Peter Brueckner recently grabbed a camera, hopped into his Skyline and headed for the Santa Cruz mountains.  

Peter's a camera engineer by trade, and his weekend toy is this 1971 Nissan Skyline 2000GTX.

Ground Control helped build a custom pair of coilovers utilising Koni shocks. For the rear, Peter has fitted RS Start adjustable perches and springs. Peter's a self confessed Watanabe fan, but he decided on a set of Hayashi Street CRs instead. The fronts are 15×7.5 with a +6 offset, while the rears are a tasty 9.5 wide with a -15 offset.

He may have gone with the Hayashi rims, but Peter still gave some love to Watanabe via a Falcon steering wheel.

Currently the L20 only has Mikuni 40mm side drafts, but one of Peter's new year's resolutions is to drop in a larger L motor.

Keep up the good work Peter!

- Charles Kha
