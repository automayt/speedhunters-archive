Random Snap&gt;&gt; Wanders Rb26 Powered S14

January 4, 2012 TheSpeedhunters

One of the most extreme S14s I know is this one owned by Lennard Wander. I spotted this in 2009 and I still think it’s one the coolest S14s in the world. Not only is it extreme on the outside thanks to a BN Sports blister kit, but under the hood lurks something equally impressive.

That custom exhaust is connected to a fully built RB26 that delivers more than enough to get the car sideways in spectacular fashion. I last saw it at the 24hrS Nürburgring race where it was doing demonstration runs on track.

-Jeroen Willemsen
