Video Roll&gt;&gt;koguchi 180 Style

January 3, 2012 TheSpeedhunters

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ewHUsq3SP-I

Since it’s S13 day and we’ve been asked to select our favorite S13, there was really no way I could pick anything but Yoshinori Koguchi’s black 180SX. This car had and still has the perfect style in my eye, and it’s made all that much better by Koguchi’s talents behind the wheel. This famous clip of him driving at Nikko Circuit just sums it all up.

It also takes you back to a simpler time when the only real way to experience drifting for most of us was through imported Japanese video tapes.

I just wonder how many people went out and bought an S13 after seeing this clip?

-Mike Garrett
