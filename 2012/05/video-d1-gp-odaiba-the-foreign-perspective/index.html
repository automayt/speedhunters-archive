Video&gt;&gt; D1-gp Odaiba - The Foreign Perspective

May 1, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Have you ever wondered what it might be like to enter a D1 Grand Prix round and compete against all those famous Japanese drifters? Well a bunch of foreigners did just that at the first round of the year in Odaiba earlier this month. Luke Huxham of Maiham Media was in Tokyo during the weekend and decided to drop by and check the action out. He put this quick mini-documentary together talking to Mad Mike, Federico Sceriffo and Jake Jones about their time at the event and their take on the whole drift scene. It’s an interesting angle on D1, a refreshing shift of perspective from the usual coverage we are used to seeing. Hit play and enjoy!
