Wrt X Speedhunters Audi R8 Desktops

May 18, 2012 Paddy McGrath

It’s around midday here at the Nürburgring and the sun is shining once again. The 24HR cars are just completing their second qualifying sessions but I’m inside preparing for the ADAC Nürburgring Classic race which takes place in about 90 minutes times. Whilst I’m waiting for some batteries to charge, I thought I’d upload a couple of desktops from yesterday’s session of the WRT Speedhunters Audi R8. If you want to keep up with the race, I’ve  included the links from Jonathan’s story including live timing, GPS tracking and live streams (which may be region specific).
