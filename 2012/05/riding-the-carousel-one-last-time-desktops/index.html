Riding The Carousel One Last Time: Desktops

May 27, 2012 Jonathan Moore

Before we round up our stories from the 2012 Nürburgring 24 Hours here’s a final batch of desktops.

To close off the coverage we’ll have an insider’s look at the race week coming up courtesy of Speedhunters WRT Audi driver Edward Sandström.

Edward will be taking us through the Nürburgring 24 Hours from his arrival at the track, the team’s efforts in practice and qualifying and then the tough 24 hours the #4 car endured during the race itself.

Already we’re all dreaming of next year’s enduro at the Nordschleife – though there are plenty more round-the-clockers coming up over the coming months to keep us off the streets and on the tracks…

Jonathan Moore
