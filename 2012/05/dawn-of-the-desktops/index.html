Dawn Of The Desktops

May 20, 2012 Paddy McGrath

George A. Romero references aside, the earth has finally turned just enough on its axis to allow the sun to shine on the Eifel region once more. For the sleep deprived, the darkness seemed to last an eternity. At 05:38AM the ball of fire poked its head about the horizon but with it came another twist in the story – heavy rain. Varied conditions have provided some drama to an event that certainly wasn’t lacking in it in the first place. I’ll let Jonathan fill you in on the details shortly …
