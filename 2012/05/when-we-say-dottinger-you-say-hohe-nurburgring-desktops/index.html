When We Say Döttinger, You Say Höhe... Nürburgring Desktops

May 18, 2012 Sean Klingelhoefer

The Nürburgring is an amazing circuit that surely needs no introduction, and it’s as difficult as it is rewarding not only for drivers but also photographers. Trudging through the mud, slipping and sliding and hiking through the woods and going up and down extreme grades can be tiring, but the pay-off is always undoubtedly worth it. The Nordschleife rewards the brave and virtually all of its hundreds of corners are picture perfect. Regardless of lens or camera it’s nearly impossible to take a bad shot here.
