D1-sl East Nikko&gt;&gt; The Battles

April 3, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Each of the 46 entrants at the D1 Street Legal East in Nikko this past Sunday got three qualifying runs to show Koguchi, and the rest of the judges, if they were good enough to make it into the top 16. 

For those that made it through it was a scramble to get ready for the tsuiso rounds that would follow, checking their cars over thoroughly, sticking on a fresh set of rear rubber and patiently waiting for the most exciting part of the day to begin.

Before getting started each driver is presented to the crowds, here is Andrew Gray driving by the judges as Naoto Suenaga hangs out the passenger side!

The first battle of the day was between Kenichi Takahashi in the green ken Works JZX100 and Nobuhisa Sone in the Sone Auto S15, respectively the top and last qualifiers in the qualifying runs. Sone took the win and moved on to the Best 8.

Andrew Gray’s Best 16 bout was against Norio Saitou where he managed to not only put a lot of distance from the red S13 on his leading run…

…but stay glued to his rear bumper when chasing for a well deserved win.

Here is Ootani in his Evolex Link S15 disposing of Ikeda in the yellow S14. He may have not been able to keep close on corner entry…

…but did a good job catching up and in his leading run.

Yuuji Saitou may not have the most powerful car in the D1-SL circuit, but what his 380 HP S15 lacks in outright numbers it makes up in response and agility as he perfectly demonstrated in his Best 16 battle against Hamakawa’s S13. Saitou stormed through to the Best 8 where he beat Kawamura and his PS13 and then took the win in the semi-finals against Kawashima in the Makeover 180SX.

Andrew Gray’s road to the final called for some hard fights, as he met up with some tough opponents like Sone in Best 8. D1-GP drivers Koguchi and Takayama, who were judging, had a hard time choosing the better driver and called for a “one more time” and then a “sudden death” for a total of six runs from the drivers! Andy had the upper hand and went up…

…against Ootani in the Semi final. Here he is showing what “close” looks like when chasing your opponent.

It was then on to last battle of the day, Gray vs Saitou and both of these guys were driving at 110%. The first run was so close that a “one more time” was called.

Andy accidentally touched the rear of the S15 as they were building up speed before the first corner entry, not wanting Saitou to get ahead. Saitou wasn’t too happy about the contact and protested to the judges.

It all ended with a “Sudden Death” run where Saitou had a better advantage on the first part of the run…

…allowing him to grab the win. It was a very hard fought final just like the battles you see in D1 Grand Prix so hats off to both drivers.

Saitou seemed relieved that the battle was over as he sure had to fight for the win.

He was handed the trophy by Koguchi…

…as well as given the 1st Place prize of ¥70,000 ($845) and some BN Sports aero parts.

Andy was happy with his second place finish and was already looking forward to the next round in May in his home track of Ebisu.

Here is the battle scar that Andy’s big JZX100 inflicted on the rear end of the Saitou’s S15…

…and the tire marks the Silvia left behind on the side of the Mark II on the final run of the day.
