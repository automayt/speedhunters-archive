Car Spotlight&gt;&gt; Vip Majesta On Ssrs

April 3, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Here is another great sighting from the Nikko parking lot during Sunday’s D1 Street Legal round. It’s not only drift cars you come across at events like these…

…like this Crown Majesta perfectly shows us. This VIP-ed sedan was riding on a set of 19-inch SSR Professors SP1s.

There was no onikyan to be seen, just good old-fashioned aggressive negative camber at the rear and custom metal widened fenders to contain the massive offset of the wheels.

Pretty hot from every angle wouldn’t you agree?

Tires measured 265/35R19 at the rear and judging from the amount of stretch the SSRs were probably a good 11-12J wide!
