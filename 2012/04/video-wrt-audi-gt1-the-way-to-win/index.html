Video&gt;&gt; Wrt Audi Gt1: The Way To Win

April 12, 2012 Jonathan Moore

Winning on the track isn’t just about rocking up with a couple of cars and putting the hour in on track for the race: days of planning and round-the-clock preparation is put in by teams before race weekends, and then hours of analysis and set-up work undertaken between the track sessions.

WRT have put together this cool video of their hugely successful weekend in the opener for the 2012 FIA World GT1 Championship at Nogaro, which gives an insider view of the whole team’s effort during the event. You can also catch up with the highlights of the Championship Race here.
