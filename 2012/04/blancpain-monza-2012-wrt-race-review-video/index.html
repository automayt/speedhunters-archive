Blancpain Monza 2012&gt;&gt; Wrt Race Review Video

April 19, 2012 Jonathan Moore

The WRT Audi team have put together this excellent review of their race in Monza, which demonstrates the hard work of the whole team over their rainy weekend in Italy. In particular, check out the effort required by the drivers to keep the cars on track in the pouring rain while they’re circulating behind the safety car at the start of the race. The visibility was virtually nil – as was the grip! Another great clip from the team, who are continuing their hectic schedule by trucking across Europe to take part in this weekend’s GT1 round in Belgium. It never stops…

Jonathan Moore
