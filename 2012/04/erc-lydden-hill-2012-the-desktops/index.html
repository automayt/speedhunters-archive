Erc Lydden Hill 2012&gt;&gt; The Desktops

April 15, 2012 Ross I&#039;Anson

To finish up our coverage from the Monster Energy Rallycross festival. We bring you some hi-res desktop images from the rain and mud of Lydden Hill.

First up is Liam Doran on his way to an epic win during his first heat.

Tanner foust kicking up the shale on his way to the overall win.

Ollie O’Donovan taking the wide line though the final turn.

Enough mud and shale to send your lens back to the repairers.

And finally Liam Doran slithering onto the tarmac to take 3rd place overall. Enjoy.

Ross I’Anson.
