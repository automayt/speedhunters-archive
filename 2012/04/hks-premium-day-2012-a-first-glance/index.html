Hks Premium Day 2012&gt;&gt; A First Glance

April 22, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

I’m getting tired of rain. Over the last few weeks I’ve had to endure far too many rainy days, and while shooting in the wet can have its advantages, it didn’t do anything for today’s proceedings. You see when you are trying to put on a track event, with time-attack sessions thrown in for extra entertainment, a slippery surface is not exactly what you need…which is why we all went home a little earlier than planned form this year’s HKS Premium Day.

Still, the rain didn’t come until the afternoon, so it was far from a wasted day. I’ve got lots to show you from what has quickly become one of the most important days on the Japanese “tuning event calendar.” On top of HKS promoting all its cool demo cars, like the brand new 86…

…the guys at Option Magazine put on the “Super Lap” time attack session where some of the country’s most respected and notorious tuning shops come out for a crack at setting a fast lap around Fuji Speedway.

All the big names participate, like Pan Speed, even if their wide body FD3S didn’t make it out on track today.

RE Wing came up from Mie-ken with their very capable street-registered RX-7.

One of the biggest attractions of the Premium Day is the Hyper Challenge, where privateers can take their cars on track in a series of timed outings. This is one little line up of Imprezas in the paddock.

There was so much goodness in the pits this year, cars that we haven’t seen before like this Garage Ito BNR34 all the way from Osaka.

Respected Evo specialist Garage G Force  showed up with quite the selection of cars which on top of a few customer cars…

…included their new EvoX demo car running a very mean wide body kit and a set of all-new Volk Racing G25s.

They even had an 86 in the special 86/BRZ display area that was set up. Garage G Force, now working out of a new workshop in Yokohama, are beginning to expand and work on non-Evo cars too, like the R35 and of course the 86.

You can always count on Mizota-san at Revolve S.A. to come up with something cool and original. This is the new Z34 demo car and one to watch for sure!

The Sawa brothers of Auto Select never miss the Premium Day, each year trying to better their time at Fuji in the Yellow Shark R34 demo car. In case you are wondering, it’s as fast and loud as it is wild looking!

There is plenty of action to go through, form both the Option Fuji Superlap and the R35 GT-R Fuji Speedmax sessions…

…not to mention a look at all the cars on display in the pits & paddock. This is only a taste of what’s to come so check back soon for more.

HKS

-Dino Dalle Carbonare
