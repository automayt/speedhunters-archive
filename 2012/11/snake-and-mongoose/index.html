The Snake And The Mongoose Movie Trailer

November 19, 2012 Mike Garrett

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zKgklgTW-7A

The story of drag racers Don “The Snake” Prudhomme and Tom “The Mongoose” McEwen is a great one, and next year that story will be coming to the big screen in the form “The Snake and the Mongoose”. The independent film is currently in postproduction and as this trailer shows, it looks like they’ve nailed the period set pieces and vehicles, thanks in large part to Don Prudhomme’s fantastic car collection.

It looks promising, and I’m certainly anxious to see it once it’s released in 2013.

-Mike
