#iamthespeedhunter: The First Theme

November 28, 2012 Paddy McGrath

With the increase in popularity of #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER, I thought it might be interesting to mix things up a little bit. Before I go any further, I want to emphasise that this isn’t a competition. Rather, this is about something we can never have too much of in our lives – fun. Let’s try and make this a weekly thing that not only inspires us all to get out and take photographs, but something we can all talk about afterwards.

For the first few themes, I’m going to try and keep things simple but interesting. You don’t need to have thousands of dollars / euros / pounds /  (insert applicable currency here) worth of equipment to take part, just a keen eye and the ability to think creatively. Since this is Speedhunters, the subject of your photograph should be based around the vast world of automotive.

The first theme is Colour.

Okay it’s a little clichéd but it’s actually a little bit more difficult than you think and each theme is completely open to interpretation.

If you have any questions, please feel free to ask them below. If you’re submitting to the theme, please ensure that you include ‘Theme’ in the subject line of the submitted e-mail, or the hashtag #IATSTHEME on Instagram, Twitter etc. Depending on how popular this proves, it might be run as a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly theme. I’ll also be listening to suggestions below for future themes …

–

#IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER

About #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER

We created #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER to allow you the opportunity to share your skills and car culture experiences from around the globe with the rest of the Speedhunters audience.

How do you get involved? It’s simple…

Flickr - Join our #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER Flickr Group and share your favourite photos with us and others.
Instagram – Follow us on Instagram at @TheSpeedhunters and tag your own car culture images with #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER
Twitter – Follow us on Twitter at @SPEEDHUNTERS and share your tweets with the #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER hashtag.
Tumblr – Visit our Tumblr page to view the latest #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER tagged images.
E-Mail – If you’ve been to a cool event and captured some amazing images, be sure to e-mail them to us oniamthespeedhunter@dev.speedhunters.com and tell us a little bit about the event. The images need to be a minimum of 800×533 pixels.
