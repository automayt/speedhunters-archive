Riding On The Back Of Audi&#039;s Winning Ways

November 14, 2012 Jonathan Moore

Automotive film-maker, Stephane Benini has produced this short video artfully covering their championship-winning season in the inaugural FIA World Endurance Championship.. It uses a whole load of Steadicams, which in itself isn’t unusual – except here they’re mounted on people’s backs, giving a unique perspective on the action from the views of drivers, team personnel, photographers and fans. It’s quite a ride!

Jonathan Moore
