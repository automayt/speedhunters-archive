Vaughn Gittin Jr Brings It Home

October 17, 2012 Jonathan Moore

Vaughn might have just missed out on this year’s Formula Drift title, but this clip celebrates the drift car he’s made his own: the Mustang. Ford let Vaughn loose around their Flat Rock Assembly Plant where every Mustang that’s rolled off the production line since 2004 has been constructed.

Deciding to give his Mustang its own test drive, Vaughn then gives us a tour of the Ford facility in the only way he knows how: sideways and at full throttle! I bet there were some very nervous looking Ford employees watching this as he slalomed the parked-up production cars… Awesome stuff.

Jonathan Moore
