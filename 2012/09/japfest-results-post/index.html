The Dust Settles At Japfest

September 9, 2012 Larry Chen

I can remember so many epic battles after shooting drifting for over eight years, and the battle between Daijiro Yoshihara and James Deane today will be forever added to my list of all top tandem runs. It was just utterly amazing to see these two top drifters go at it around the challenging and technical Mondello Park drift course.

The day was literally action packed as qualifying, super lap and top 16 were all in a single day. James Deane qualified 1st and of course he went on to win the super lap, which is a round that pits all the top 16 drivers against each other for one final run to decide final t0p 16 running order.

There were also a fair amount of upsets too, as local favorite Darren McNamara did not make the top 16 due to mechanical issues. The team had to disable the power steering as it was just not working.

The late fixes were not enough to make the car competitive though. Hopefully Darren will be able to put all these troubles behind him as Formula Drift Irwindale is just around the corner.

Fredric qualified 5th and into the top 16 with ease although he was also having troubles with the car setup. The Hammer was having a difficult event as his e-brake stopped functioning during his qualifying runs.

In the super lap round he went in way too hot and ended up off course. He was then given 8th, which meant he was paired up with ProDrift points leader Brendan Stone.

Both drivers battled very hard and Fredric had excellent proximity. Unfortunately, he put two tires off course on his chase run which gave Brendan the win.

By beating Aasbo and making it in the top 8, Brendan had enough points to win the 2012 ProDrift Championship.

The final battle was between Daijiro Yoshihara and the new ProDrift Champion.

The battle was very close, and resulted in a One-More-Time. Unfortunately, Daijiro’s Altezza had some mechanical issues. It was leaking coolant and the drive shaft was loose so they had to call  five minutes to fix as much as they could.

With the time ticking down the team only had a chance to address one of the problems and they figured it was a huge safety issue to have a loose drive shaft. Ultimately, Dai understeered on his follow run which gave Brendan the win.

This was Dai’s second time competing in ProDrift. Last year he was knocked out of the top 8 by Dmac, but this year he did much better.

This was Brendan’s first drift championship and I’m sure it will be one of many to come. Who knows, maybe we will see him competing in Formula Drift one day.

After the event,we interviewed the guest FD drivers about their event. You can bet we recorded everything. Keep an eye out for and interview with the three stooges.

That is it for now.
