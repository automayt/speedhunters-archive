2012 Speedhunters Awards: Video Of The Year

December 28, 2012 Mike Garrett

Now for a new addition to the Speedhunters Awards – Video of the Year. With the vast amount of high quality automotive films being released these days, we only saw fitting to include a category for you guys to pick the video of the year. You can think of it as the Speedhunters version of the Oscars.

Here are the nominees –

Magnus Walker: Urban Outlaw

A detailed look at Mr. Magnus Walker and his deep obsession for Porsche 911s.

Vaughn Gittin Jr. 2nd Shift Drift

Released by Ford Racing, this film features Vaughn Gittin Jr. doing some rather crazy things with his drift car at the Mustang factory.

WRT Audi at the Green Hell

Simple and to the point, an on-board view from the Speedhunters WRT Audi as it tackles the Green Hell.

Racing In The Street : Group C Porsche 962

One of many great films this year from Luke Huxham done as a collabo with Motorhead Magazine. It’s a street driven Porsche 962 and that’s all you really need to know.

O’snap – Gatebil: The Game Changer

I doubt there’s any film that can fully sum up the insanity of Gatebil, but Daniel Hovdahl’s video does a pretty solid job.

OLDER N’ FASTER // THE CALIFORNIA LOOK

Here we have film maker Stephen Brooks doing a fine job of investigating the Cal Look VW movement and its history.

Bloodmasters: Bloodbath Pt.1

The pure madness of East Coast style drifting.

Ken Block’s Gymkhana Five

Ken Block manages to outdo himself once again with this romp through the streets of San Francisco.

Porsche in Le Mans – The 70s

A short but enjoyable collection of retro footage from Porsche. Can’t go wrong with vintage!

The Rolex 24 Experience

This film shows driver Leh Keen’s perspective of the Rolex 24. Not just behind the wheel, but everything that comes with it.

Depth of Speed: My Church

Any of the films from Josh Clason’s Depth of Speed series is worthy of being on this list. This one profiles one fellow and his love of Datsuns.

Le WEC : une compétition, mille expériences

Stephane Benini’s very unusual, and very touching take on Audi’s WEC season.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVsN83euv5M

From Birth to Monaco

A brilliant piece by the BBC F1 showing the first-person journey from birth to F1 stardom.

Keep Drifting Fun

One of the most anticipated films of the year, Will Roegge and Joshua Herron capture the essence of grassroots drifting in America.
