#iamthespeedhunter: Weekly Round-up

December 5, 2012 Paddy McGrath

I hope you’re feeling inspired after yesterday’s story by John Zhang, I know I am. Just in case though, here is this week’s round up of some of the best images submitted to #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER over the last seven days. First up if Justin Marlow’s super slow shutter shot of this sliding 350Z.

Heading over to Instagram, and @toantech tagged this image of a 240Z shot from an on the floor perspective.

Sticking with IG for a moment, we can grab a peak at the front of this E9x M3 from Romania.

Our new Flickr group has really been supplying the goods as of late. Otis Blank composed this gorgeous shot of some fine Scandinavian metal at the Revolvstore in Tucson, Arizona.

I’ve not forgotten our colour theme either, the submissions are still coming in so I’m going to keep it open for another week.

Sometimes, you just have to be in the right place and at the right time. I can’t wait to see these driven in anger in next year’s WRC.

GT3 plus the Nordschleife equals bliss. A simple automotive mathematical equation.

Luke Huxham released this through the @maihammedia Instagram account – a straight from the camera shot of the RWB Rough Unity. Beautiful.

Eric Delaney was on hand to capture Ken Block overcooking it into a tight left hander at a recent event. It’s one thing being in the right place, it’s another to be able to do something about it.

I’d recognise that scrutiny bay anywhere. Taken at this year’s Japfest event in Mondello, Conor Luddy captured a brief moment of quiet time for this E46.

There are plenty of submissions coming in from the LA Autoshow too. I’ve said it before but it’s a great way of checking out an event if you can’t be there.

With IATS, your submissions don’t need to be the most recent shots you have taken. A good photo will always be a good photo.

Whether you’re out with friends …

… or attending a top class event, always try to shoot as much as you can.

If McLaren are getting in on the #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER action, then you have no excuse not to join them.

–

#IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER

About #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER

We created #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER to allow you the opportunity to share your skills and car culture experiences from around the globe with the rest of the Speedhunters audience.

How do you get involved? It’s simple…

Flickr - Join our #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER Flickr Group and share your favourite photos with us and others.
Instagram – Follow us on Instagram at @TheSpeedhunters and tag your own car culture images with #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER
Twitter – Follow us on Twitter at @SPEEDHUNTERS and share your tweets with the #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER hashtag.
Tumblr – Visit our Tumblr page to view the latest #IAMTHESPEEDHUNTER tagged images.
E-Mail – If you’ve been to a cool event and captured some amazing images, be sure to e-mail them to us on iamthespeedhunter@dev.speedhunters.com and tell us a little bit about the event. The images need to be a minimum of 800×533 pixels.

Have you submitted already but yet been featured? Here are some tips to help you…

DO – Submit your best work, regardless of when it was taken.
DO – Take your time shooting and consider each detail
DO – Try to submit each image no larger than 1000PX wide.
DON’T – Put a huge watermark on it. We’ll make sure you’re credited!
DON’T – Send us huge image files that kill our e-mail system!
