Tsukuba Obsession: Under Suzuki And His S15

December 9, 2012 Dino Dalle Carbonare

Under Suzuki has redefined perseverance. He has gone head to head against the best and has come out victorious. The 53.6 sec lap he set back on Friday is merely another goal he has reached. Where he will stop, nobody knows. Luke Huxham of Maiham Media met up with Suzuki-san earlier this year after a Battle Evome event and chatted to him about his goals for the future. Hit play, sit back and bow to what is quickly becoming quite the legend in time attack circles…
