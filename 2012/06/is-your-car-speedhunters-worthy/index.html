Is Your Car Speedhunters Worthy?

June 6, 2012 Mike Garrett

Over the last couple months we’ve rolled out numerous changes to improve the Speedhunters experience. Whether it’s delivering more of the content you guys love, expanding our Speedhunting horizons, or improving the functionality of the site itself, we’ve been working non-stop to make Speedhunters better and better.

Since the beginning, reader rides and other reader-sourced material have been a crucial part of the Speedhunters sphere. In order to streamline this process and bring more of this content to the world, we have established a new way for you guys to submit material to the site.

While we would love to physically get out there and shoot all of these great stories from across the world , budget and time restraints make this an impossibility. That doesn’t mean that they don’t deserve to be shared tough.

If you have a cool car or build story that you think is worthy of being featured on Speedhunters, drop us a line at featurethis@dev.speedhunters.com . Please make sure to include your name and location, as well as some basic details and specs, along with at least 5-10 photographs. Photos should be no less than 800 pixels wide.

Keep in mind we are looking for truly “Speedhunters-worthy” vehicles. Odd builds, wild resto projects, sleepers, race cars, crazy engine swaps, cars doing things they were never designed for, etc.

We plan on choosing the most interesting vehicles and build stories for a series of posts that will become a regular part of the site.

So, if you or someone you know has a car that needs to be seen by the world, send it in!

-Mike Garrett
