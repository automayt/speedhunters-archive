The Weekend is Here. Let&#039;s Hunt Some Speed!

June 8, 2012 Rob Bullough

First of all, well done to everyone who entered our first Speedhunting Weekend. We had an overwhelming response and there was some quality hardware on show in the images that were posted up. Our heroic winners (@indigoindica, @rizalwebb, @uncle_toby, @blueyesss and @mrderek) from last week are now all the proud owners of some very exclusive Speedhunters swag.

This week we are once again looking for you all to channel your inner Speedhunter – we want to see the world through your lens. The theme is to get yourself to an automotive event this weekend (a meet, show, race… anything) and shoot it. Post your pics to Instagram or Twitter using the hashtag #SpeedhuntingWKND. Entries will be accepted up to midnight PST on Sunday June 10 (6am GMT, 7am CET Monday June 11).

The best five entries will win Speedhunters stickers and other assorted awesomeness that we find in the hallowed cupboard of wonder and plenty. You know the drill.

Speedhunting Weekend 02 is now officially open.
